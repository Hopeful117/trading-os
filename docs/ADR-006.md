# ADR-006 — Market Data Service Responsibilities

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-23

## Context

Trading OS requires a reliable source of market information that is independent from broker-specific implementations.

Market information is consumed by multiple subsystems:

- Trading Core
- AI Engine
- Passive Scanner
- Active Scanner
- Frontend
- Future analytics services

Originally, market synchronization was limited to importing available markets from a broker.

As the platform evolved, it became clear that Market Data Service should become the central provider of market intelligence rather than a simple synchronization service.

## Decision

Market Data Service becomes the authoritative source of market-related information.

It owns:

- market catalog;
- market metadata;
- market constraints;
- market state;
- realtime market streams.

It is intentionally independent from Trading Core business logic.

---

## Responsibilities

The service is responsible for:

- synchronizing markets from providers;
- maintaining market reference data;
- exposing market information;
- managing realtime subscriptions;
- publishing market events.

Business decisions remain outside this service.

---

## Market Ownership

Market Data Service owns the Market entity.

Trading Core consumes market information but never owns or persists market data.

The Market entity represents a tradable financial instrument independently of any broker-specific API.

---

## Market State

Trading OS distinguishes market metadata from market availability.

A dedicated embedded MarketState object represents whether trading is currently possible.

A market is considered **OPEN** only when orders can be executed immediately.

Examples considered CLOSED include:

- pre-market sessions;
- maintenance periods;
- suspended markets.

This definition avoids misleading states where trading is technically unavailable despite market preparation activities.

---

## Market Constraints

Each market owns immutable trading constraints such as:

- minimum order size;
- minimum cost;
- tick size;
- quantity precision;
- price precision.

These constraints are synchronized from providers and consumed by Trading Core.

---

## Market Synchronization

Synchronization is responsible for:

- discovering new markets;
- updating market metadata;
- updating MarketState;
- updating trading constraints.

Synchronization does not remove historical markets automatically.

Reference information is refreshed periodically.

---

## Realtime Market Data

Realtime prices are obtained through provider WebSocket connections.

The service converts provider-specific messages into provider-independent domain events.

Example:

```
Kraken Ticker
        │
        ▼
MarketDataEvent
```

Provider DTOs must never leave the provider layer.

---

## Dynamic Subscriptions

Realtime subscriptions are managed dynamically.

The system should not permanently subscribe to every available market.

Subscriptions may be requested by:

- frontend market detail pages;
- Passive Scanner;
- Active Scanner;
- Position Monitoring Agent.

Unused subscriptions should eventually be released.

---

## Event Model

Provider-specific payloads are mapped into provider-independent domain events.

Example:

```
Kraken DTO

↓

MarketDataEvent

↓

Consumers
```

Consumers remain completely independent from Kraken's protocol.

---

## Frontend Responsibilities

Market Data Service exposes market catalog APIs consumed by frontend applications.

Examples include:

- market list;
- market detail;
- realtime market updates.

The service remains responsible only for market information.

It does not expose business calculations.

---

## Consequences

Advantages:

- single source of truth;
- provider-independent domain model;
- reusable market information;
- scalable realtime architecture;
- simplified Trading Core.

Trade-offs:

- dedicated synchronization lifecycle;
- WebSocket subscription management;
- provider adapter maintenance.

These trade-offs are accepted.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### Market Data Inside Broker Service

Rejected.

Broker connectivity and market intelligence evolve independently.

Keeping them separated improves maintainability.

---

### Trading Core Owns Market Data

Rejected.

Trading Core consumes market information but should not become responsible for maintaining market metadata.

---

### Permanent WebSocket Subscriptions

Rejected.

Maintaining subscriptions for every market unnecessarily increases infrastructure costs and complexity.

Dynamic subscriptions provide a better balance between responsiveness and resource usage.
