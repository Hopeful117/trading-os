# ADR-015 — Product-Driven Architecture

- **Status:** Accepted**
- **Date:** 2026-07-23

## Context

Trading OS is intended to become a long-term intelligent trading platform.

Throughout the project, numerous implementation decisions could have been driven by:

- broker APIs;
- market data providers;
- AI capabilities;
- frontend technologies.

However, allowing external technologies to dictate the system architecture would progressively distort the product vision.

The platform should instead evolve from its business objectives and product philosophy.

Technology exists to support the product rather than define it.

---

## Decision

Trading OS adopts a Product-Driven Architecture.

Business objectives always take precedence over implementation details.

The architecture should evolve according to the needs of the trading workflow rather than the capabilities or limitations of external providers.

---

## Product First

Every new feature should answer the following question:

> Does this improve the trader's ability to understand markets, manage risk or make better trading decisions?

If the answer is negative, the feature should be reconsidered.

---

## Domain Before APIs

Business concepts are designed before provider integrations.

Examples include:

- Market
- MarketState
- Trade
- Position
- Trading Rule Profile
- Market Intelligence

Providers adapt to the domain.

The domain never adapts to providers.

---

## AI Serves the Product

Artificial Intelligence exists to improve trader productivity.

AI should:

- reduce cognitive load;
- explain market context;
- rank opportunities;
- summarize information;
- assist decision making.

AI should never exist merely because a model is available.

---

## Market Data Collection

Trading OS should collect only information providing measurable value.

Typical examples include:

- market constraints;
- realtime prices;
- historical candles;
- economic context;
- technical observations.

Provider-specific information without business value should not become part of the domain model.

---

## Incremental Evolution

Trading OS is expected to evolve continuously.

The first complete version represents the beginning of the product rather than its final state.

Future improvements should primarily originate from real trading experience.

User feedback is considered more valuable than speculative feature development.

---

## Long-Term Vision

The platform should progressively evolve into an intelligent Trading Operating System capable of assisting the user throughout the complete trading lifecycle.

This includes:

- market preparation;
- opportunity discovery;
- trade validation;
- position monitoring;
- post-trade analysis;
- continuous learning.

---

## Design Principles

Future architectural decisions should prioritize:

- business value;
- simplicity;
- explainability;
- maintainability;
- extensibility.

Technology choices should remain secondary.

---

## Consequences

Advantages:

- coherent product evolution;
- stable business architecture;
- easier prioritization;
- reduced feature creep;
- long-term maintainability.

Trade-offs:

- some provider capabilities may intentionally remain unused;
- additional abstraction layers may be required.

These trade-offs are accepted.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### Provider-Driven Development

Rejected.

External APIs should not determine the internal architecture.

---

### Technology-Driven Roadmap

Rejected.

New technologies should be adopted only when they strengthen the product vision.
