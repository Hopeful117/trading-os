# ADR-012 — Domain-Driven Market Modeling

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-23

## Context

Trading OS integrates multiple external providers including brokers and market data vendors.

Each provider exposes its own API, terminology, identifiers and business model.

Examples include:

- Kraken
- Binance
- Interactive Brokers
- Future providers

Building the internal domain model directly from provider APIs would tightly couple the platform to external implementations and make future integrations increasingly difficult.

The objective of Trading OS is to model the trading domain rather than individual provider APIs.

## Decision

Trading OS adopts a Domain-Driven approach for market modeling.

Business entities are designed according to Trading OS business needs.

External provider models are considered implementation details.

Every provider must therefore map its data into the Trading OS domain model.

---

## Domain First

The internal model is driven by business concepts such as:

- Market
- MarketState
- MarketConstraints
- Account
- Trade
- Position

These concepts exist independently of any broker implementation.

---

## Provider Adapters

Every provider is responsible for translating its own DTOs into Trading OS domain objects.

Example:

```
KrakenAssetPairDto

↓

KrakenMarketMapper

↓

Market
```

The mapper belongs to the provider layer.

No provider DTO may leave this boundary.

---

## Business Independence

Business services consume only Trading OS domain models.

Examples:

Trading Core consumes:

- Market
- Account
- Position

never:

- KrakenAccountDto
- KrakenTickerDto
- BinanceSymbolDto

---

## Data Collection Philosophy

Trading OS does not attempt to collect every piece of data exposed by providers.

Only information providing measurable business value should be integrated.

Typical examples include:

- market constraints;
- tradability;
- realtime prices;
- historical candles;
- economic context.

Unnecessary provider-specific fields should not become part of the domain model.

---

## Evolution

The domain model may evolve independently from providers.

Adding a new provider should primarily require:

- a new adapter;
- new mappers;
- provider configuration.

Existing business services should require little or no modification.

---

## Design Principles

The domain model should remain:

- provider-independent;
- business-oriented;
- stable;
- reusable;
- easy to understand.

Provider APIs are considered volatile.

Business concepts are considered stable.

---

## Consequences

Advantages:

- loose coupling;
- simpler future integrations;
- cleaner business model;
- easier testing;
- easier long-term maintenance.

Trade-offs:

- additional mapping layer;
- duplicated provider fields when necessary.

These trade-offs are accepted.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### Model Everything From Broker APIs

Rejected.

Provider APIs are implementation details and should not define the business domain.

---

### Share Provider DTOs Between Services

Rejected.

DTOs belong exclusively to the provider layer.

Only domain objects should circulate inside Trading OS.
