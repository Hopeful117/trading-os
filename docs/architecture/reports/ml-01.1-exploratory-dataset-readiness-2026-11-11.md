# ML-01.1 - September 2026 Exploratory Dataset Readiness

## 1. Research Objective

Execute the existing Java HistData pipeline on the September 2026 EUR/USD and
GBP/USD sources and determine whether the resulting exploratory population can
be presented for an ML-01.2 scientific go/no-go decision.

This was a research experiment only. No production functionality, Engineering
Story, ADR, model training, KMeans execution, broker operation or historical
download was performed.

The existing eligibility rule reduces these to 190 EUR/USD and 162 GBP/USD
jointly eligible rows. Unavailable outcomes were primarily caused by
`UNEXPLAINED_GAP` continuity rejection and incomplete forward windows. The
latest rows also fail when the requested forward horizon is not present.

Available outcomes use exact observation-count semantics: H+4, H+12 and H+24
valid H1 candles. Their elapsed durations are normally four, twelve and
twenty-four hours in contiguous segments; elapsed durations increase when
unresolved gaps are crossed in rows that are still reported diagnostically.

The exported outcome schema does not contain a dedicated reopening price-jump
field. Reopening transition provenance contains classifications and elapsed
durations, but price-jump analysis would require a separate deterministic
projection or direct source-level calculation and was not invented here.

## 9. Chronological Partition And Leakage Assessment

The run used `PartitionConfiguration.requiresApproval()`. The partition
manifest therefore reports:

- `REQUIRES_SCIENTIFIC_APPROVAL`;
- no train, validation or test boundaries;
- zero rows assigned to partitions;
- no embargo value;
- minimum effective samples `UNAPPROVED`.

The implementation declares that future windows must not cross protected
boundaries and purges cross-boundary overlap when boundaries are supplied. It
permits and reports ordinary rolling-window overlap within a partition. This
experiment did not provide approved boundaries, purge, embargo or minimum
effective-sample parameters, so chronological train/validation/test readiness
cannot be claimed.

Rolling windows overlap heavily and must not be interpreted as independent
observations. Scaling-fit scope was not exercised by this bundle, and no
held-out test population exists in the output.

## 11. Scientific Limitations

- Only one calendar month was evaluated.
- Conflicting duplicate rows and chronology inversions are present in the raw
  source quality diagnostics.
- The assumed-transition stratum produced no valid feature window, so it cannot
  support a stratum comparison in this run.
- Partial candles represent a separate exploratory quality population and may
  be sensitive to the 55/60 admissibility threshold.
- Rolling windows create substantial dependence and reduce effective sample
  size relative to raw row counts.
- Partition, purge, embargo and minimum effective-sample parameters remain
  unapproved.
- No model performance, confidence level, statistical significance or cluster
  stability was evaluated.

## 12. Readiness Verdict

**BLOCKED_BY_DATA**

The experiment executed reproducibly, but the accepted data-integrity contract
reported quarantined conflicting duplicates and source chronology issues, and
the partition manifest has no approved train/validation/test protocol. The
population therefore cannot defensibly advance to ML-01.2 as a scientifically
ready dataset. This verdict is not a production failure and does not authorize
ML-01.2.

## 13. Recommended Next Research Action

1. Resolve or explicitly accept the source-level conflicting duplicates and
   chronology anomalies under the research governance process.
2. Obtain human approval for chronological boundaries, purge, embargo and
   minimum effective-sample requirements.
3. Re-run the same pipeline and compare fingerprints and diagnostics.
4. Decide explicitly whether the zero-observation assumed-transition stratum is
   acceptable for exploratory analysis before considering ML-01.2.
