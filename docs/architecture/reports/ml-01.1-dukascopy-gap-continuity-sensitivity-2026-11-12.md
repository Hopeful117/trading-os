# ML-01.1 Dukascopy Gap-Continuity Sensitivity

Date: 2026-11-12

Status: exploratory evidence only. No ML training, clustering, policy change, or gap closure verification was performed.

## Scope

This experiment evaluates the sensitivity of the Dukascopy 12-month M1 dataset to H1 completeness thresholds and bounded session-continuity assumptions. It uses the existing normalized BID M1 files only:

- EUR/USD: `371,614` M1 rows
- GBP/USD: `371,266` M1 rows
- Window: `[2025-10-01T00:00:00Z, 2026-10-01T00:00:00Z)`
- Dataset fingerprint: `c212d83c0050374400af8e8c3d9961a2bbcf0f9169fd2c149c0513fc3002f198`
- Manifest SHA-256: `4542e9e2ab7114edf8ca9514c146905fba8390a0753f26ba4cca47285b752041`

The experiment is a read-only sensitivity analysis. It does not replace ADR-094 or establish a new admissibility policy.

## Method

The temporary harness `/tmp/opencode/Sensitivity.java` reconstructs UTC H1 buckets, counts observed M1 rows, records first/last-minute boundary state, and computes contiguous sequences of eligible H1 buckets. It also compares structural feature windows with the existing Java `VolatilityCalculator` output for ATR-like horizons of 4, 12, and 24 H1 bars.

The scenarios are:

| Scenario | H1 rule | Session assumption |
|---|---:|---|
| A_STRICT | 60/60, first and last minute required | none |
| B_59_BOUNDARY | at least 59/60, boundaries required | none |
| C_55_BOUNDARY | at least 55/60, boundaries required | none |
| D_59_ONE_SESSION | at least 59/60, boundaries required | one bounded weekend transition allowed |
| E_55_ONE_SESSION | at least 55/60, boundaries required | one bounded weekend transition allowed |
| F_59_TWO_SESSION | at least 59/60, boundaries required | up to two bounded weekend transitions allowed |
| G_55_TWO_SESSION | at least 55/60, boundaries required | up to two bounded weekend transitions allowed |

The session rows are diagnostic only. They are not evidence that the provider schedule is known or that the gaps are safe to fill.

## Long Gaps and Candidate Sessions

Long gaps of at least 24 hours were inspected for temporal shape and candidate weekend-session compatibility. The harness found `9` candidate gaps for EUR/USD and `5` for GBP/USD. The candidate classification requires a Friday-to-Sunday UTC shape, a bounded duration, acceptable endpoint hours, and zero observed M1 rows inside the gap. This is a structural heuristic, not provider schedule evidence.

The candidate gaps were not automatically admitted under strict continuity. Allowing one or two candidate session transitions increases sequence lengths and feature-window counts, but also creates large sensitivity to an unapproved assumption. In particular, the EUR/USD 55/60 one-session scenario reaches a maximum segment of `431` H1 bars while its Java-valid 24-bar count remains `2,665`; GBP/USD reaches `407` and `1,882`, respectively.

## Findings

1. A strict 60/60 H1 policy yields no 24-bar structural feature window for either symbol under the current full-run definition.
2. A 59/60 threshold produces a small but non-zero 24-bar population: `154` EUR/USD and `14` GBP/USD structural windows without session assumptions.
3. A 55/60 threshold produces a much larger population: `2,145` EUR/USD and `1,630` GBP/USD structural windows without session assumptions.
4. The threshold choice is therefore materially outcome-determinative and cannot be treated as an implementation detail.
5. Session assumptions affect continuity topology and feature-window availability but do not alter the accepted H1 population. The one- and two-transition variants are identical on this dataset.
6. Missing minutes are predominantly clustered. Partial H1 buckets also show lower observed movement averages than full buckets, but this is descriptive and may reflect where the provider feed is absent rather than a market property.
7. There is no evidence here to classify the candidate long gaps as verified closures or to promote any policy change.

## Validation and Limits

- The dataset fingerprint and manifest checksum matched the expected values.
- The temporary harness compiled and ran with Java `21.0.12.1` and Maven `3.8.7` dependencies.
- The existing Java volatility implementation was reused for the feature comparison.
- No ML model, KMeans, purge, embargo, split, or sample-size decision was executed.
- The session detector is an exploratory heuristic and is not ADR-094 compliant evidence.
- This report does not establish that a 55/60 or 59/60 threshold is acceptable for training, validation, or production.

## Decision

No decision to close gaps, change ADR-094, change `HistDataOfflinePipeline`, or begin ML-01 training is justified by this experiment alone. The evidence supports a follow-up engineering decision on the admissibility policy, with explicit treatment of provider schedule evidence, boundary minutes, and whether partial H1 buckets may be used for structural versus Java feature computation.
