## 1. Context

Story 0139 improved the Research page frontend critical path and bounded
historical OHLC lookups. Its remaining gaps are the backend read path:

- readiness and integrity still load more persistence data than a read-only page
  necessarily needs;
- the two projections may repeat related reads;
- no measured decision has yet established whether targeted queries, a shared
  projection, or precomputation is the correct long-term design.

The page must become faster without changing readiness truth, integrity semantics,
evidence freshness, fail-closed behavior, or service ownership. A cache or
materialized projection must not silently become a new source of truth.

## 2. Decision

Research page performance improvements shall proceed in two measured backend
stages:

1. **Targeted persistence projections first.** Reduce selected row volume,
   deserialization, and duplicate reads while preserving the existing authoritative
   domain rules and response semantics.
2. **Shared or precomputed Research projection only when justified by evidence.**
   If Step 3 does not address the measured critical path, a shared projection may
   combine the independently renderable Research facts, provided its ownership,
   freshness, invalidation, failure, and rebuild semantics are explicit.

The canonical source of truth remains the existing authoritative Market
Intelligence and Market Data evidence. A projection is a read optimization and
must never authorize, reinterpret, or replace deterministic readiness rules.

## 3. Ownership and Boundary

Market Intelligence owns the Research readiness and integrity projection contract.
The frontend consumes that contract and does not aggregate separate backend facts
to produce authoritative readiness, integrity, freshness, or blocker values.

Market Data remains authoritative for market evidence. Trading Core, Broker
Service, and Risk responsibilities are not changed by this decision.

## 4. Freshness and Invalidation

Every optimized or precomputed Research value must retain explicit freshness
metadata sufficient to distinguish:

- current and authoritative;
- stale but displayable with an explicit warning;
- unavailable because the source or projection cannot be trusted.

HTTP success, cache presence, or elapsed TTL alone does not prove freshness.

If a durable or shared projection is introduced, it requires explicit invalidation
or recomputation triggers, bounded rebuild behavior, and a fail-closed response
when the projection is missing, stale beyond policy, inconsistent, or corrupted.

ADR-022 remains authoritative for cache ownership, reuse, and freshness. No
inter-request cache based only on TTL may be introduced as a substitute for
invalidation semantics.

## 5. Semantic Invariants

The optimized implementation must preserve, for identical authoritative inputs:

- readiness signal counts, thresholds, blockers, and `ready` value;
- strict evidence policy, replay, fee, OHLC, timeout, concurrency, and remote-call
  budgets;
- integrity warnings and independent failure handling;
- timestamps and freshness interpretation;
- account and authorization boundaries;
- one-request-per-endpoint behavior for a refresh;
- cold-cache and warm-cache behavior as explicitly documented.

The projection may omit internal data that is not required by the Research
contract, but it may not omit data needed to explain a blocker or freshness state.

## 6. Measurement Gate

No shared projection, materialized view, new cache authority, or persistence
ownership change is justified by intuition alone. Story 0140 must record:

- query plans and row volume before and after Step 3;
- deserialization and projection costs;
- duplicate-read and shared-read measurements;
- cold-cache and warm-cache latency;
- freshness and invalidation behavior;
- failure and recovery behavior;
- semantic equivalence against the current authoritative implementation.

If measurements do not justify a durable shared projection, the Story may close
Step 4 with an evidence-based rejection and retain targeted projections as the
final design for this performance objective.

## 7. Alternatives Rejected

### Frontend aggregation

Rejected because Angular would become responsible for deterministic readiness and
integrity semantics.

### TTL-only shared cache

Rejected because expiry does not establish dependency freshness or invalidation.

### Immediate materialized view

Rejected because it would introduce durable ownership and rebuild semantics before
the existing query and deserialization costs are measured.

### Relaxed evidence or readiness rules

Rejected because performance must not be obtained by weakening truth or safety.

## 8. Consequences

### Positive

- The remaining performance work has a controlled sequence and measurable gate.
- Backend optimization remains inside Market Intelligence boundaries.
- The Research page can gain speed without changing readiness truth.
- A durable projection, if needed, will have explicit operational semantics.

### Negative

- Measurement and semantic-equivalence work is required before a shared projection.
- Some latency may remain if the authoritative replay/fee work dominates.
- A correct result may be an evidence-based decision not to add a materialized
  projection.

## 9. Scope Boundary

This ADR does not authorize:

- a new Research product capability;
- changes to S1 thresholds, blockers, or evidence semantics;
- a new database, message broker, or generic cache technology;
- changes to service boundaries outside Market Intelligence;
- AI, analytics, backtesting, or trading recommendations;
- weakening authentication, authorization, or fail-closed behavior.
