# ADR-046 - Platform-managed planning profile for PAPER accounts

## Status

Accepted

## Context

PAPER account onboarding currently assigns an immutable versioned RiskProfile,
but it does not assign a Trade Planning Profile. The authenticated PAPER journey
therefore stops when Trading Core creates a Trade Plan because no effective
planning profile exists for the account.

RiskProfile and TradePlanningProfile remain separate authorities. The RiskProfile
defines deterministic risk-policy limits, while the TradePlanningProfile defines
the planning preferences sent to Market Intelligence.

## Decision

Every newly created PAPER account receives a new immutable version-one
TradePlanningProfile during the same transaction as PAPER account provisioning.
The profile is owned by the authenticated account owner and is assigned to the
new trading account before provisioning completes.

The platform-managed PAPER defaults are:

```text
RISK_BUDGET = initial capital * 1%
ENTRY = LIMIT
STOP = PERCENTAGE_DISTANCE at 1%
TARGET = RISK_MULTIPLE at 2R
HORIZON = INTRADAY
VALIDITY = 1 hour
```

The one-percent budget follows the approved PAPER Standard Risk Policy from
Story 0043. The planning profile is not derived by copying the RiskProfile; its
values are explicit platform defaults and retain their own immutable identity
and version.

LIVE accounts do not receive this automatic assignment. User-selected planning
profiles and later profile reassignment remain available through the existing
Trade Planning Profile contract and are outside this decision.

## Consequences

- PAPER onboarding produces an account immediately usable for Trade Plan
  creation.
- The default planning policy is explicit, versioned, and auditable.
- Existing ownership and effective-profile checks remain authoritative.
- The provisioning transaction must roll back if profile creation or assignment
  fails.
- A future user-facing planning-profile catalog can replace the default without
  changing the RiskProfile model.
