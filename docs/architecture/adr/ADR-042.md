## 1. Context

Trading OS supports two materially different execution modes:

```text
LIVE
    -> an external broker/provider owns position truth

PAPER
    -> Trading Core owns simulated financial and position truth
```

The difference is intentional. It is not an architectural inconsistency.

LIVE positions exist at a provider and can change outside Trading OS. Their
current quantity, side, exposure, provider identity, and post-mutation state
must therefore be reloaded from Broker Service and reconciled with the
provider.

PAPER positions do not exist at an external provider. They are created by
Trading Core settlement from simulated fills and must remain usable without
broker credentials or Broker Service mutation calls.

The existing position-close architecture was designed for the LIVE case. ADR-
040 defines an opaque broker position reference, provider-specific mutation
scope resolution, Broker Service execution, and provider reconciliation. Those
concepts are correct for broker-authoritative positions but do not describe a
local simulated position.

The repository currently proves persisted PAPER entry execution, but not a
complete PAPER round trip:

```text
entry -> open local position -> current valuation -> human exit
      -> realized PnL -> closed local position -> final Account state
```

This ADR establishes the boundary that future PAPER round-trip implementation
must follow. It does not implement that round trip.

## 2. Problem Statement

Without an explicit mode boundary, future work could make one of two unsafe
choices:

1. force PAPER through `PositionCloseCommand` and Broker Service, making a
   local simulation depend on provider concepts and external credentials; or
2. create an unrelated PAPER command architecture that duplicates execution,
   idempotency, risk, order, fill, audit, and human-authorization behavior.

The architecture must share the concepts that have the same business meaning
while keeping source of truth and mutation mechanics mode-specific.

The decision must also prevent a PAPER opposite-side exit from accidentally
creating a new opposite position instead of closing the selected exposure.

## 4. Decision

Trading OS SHALL maintain explicit, mode-specific position authority:

```text
LIVE_POSITION_AUTHORITY  = BROKER
PAPER_POSITION_AUTHORITY = TRADING_CORE
```

The modes SHALL share human authorization, deterministic safety, idempotency,
execution audit, and provider-neutral outcome concepts where those concepts
have the same meaning. They SHALL NOT share authority, provider mutation
mechanics, or reconciliation assumptions where those meanings differ.

## 5. LIVE Position Authority

### 5.1 Authority

For LIVE, the external broker/provider is authoritative for:

- current quantity;
- side;
- average entry basis;
- current exposure;
- provider position identity;
- open/closed state;
- post-mutation convergence.

Trading Core may project broker state for UI, risk, commands, and audit. It must
not treat a stale local projection as authoritative after a provider mutation.

### 5.2 Identity and Addressability

The broker position reference is an opaque read identity and resolution handle.
It is not necessarily the provider mutation target.

```text
read identity
    -> server-side authoritative resolution
    -> provider-supported mutation scope
```

For Kraken, ADR-040 records that a displayed txid can resolve to pair/exposure
scope with FIFO behavior. For future cTrader, a position ID can be directly
command-addressable. Trading Core must not infer one provider's addressability
from another provider's model.

### 5.3 LIVE Close

LIVE full close remains implemented through the position-management architecture
of ADR-040:

```text
human close intent
    -> ownership and fresh provider reload
    -> PositionCloseCommand
    -> Broker Service position capability
    -> provider-specific mutation
    -> ACKNOWLEDGED / REJECTED / UNKNOWN
    -> explicit provider reconciliation
```

This ADR does not redesign LIVE close, change Kraken behavior, or introduce
cTrader behavior.

## 6. PAPER Position Authority

### 6.1 Authority

Trading Core SHALL be authoritative for PAPER:

- local exposure quantity;
- side and position effect;
- entry basis from persisted fills;
- persisted open/closed lifecycle;
- simulated fills;
- financial settlement;
- realized PnL;
- restart/reload state.

Market Data MAY provide a current price and the inputs for unrealized valuation.
Market Data does not own the PAPER position lifecycle.

### 6.2 Local State

The authoritative PAPER state is local persisted Trading Core state. A future
implementation must not determine whether a PAPER position exists by calling
Broker Service or by checking external broker credentials.

The state must be reloadable after a process restart. Current price and
unrealized valuation may be a timestamped projection from current Market Data;
they must not be confused with the persisted position identity or lifecycle.

### 6.3 No External Reconciliation

PAPER has no external provider whose position state can confirm or reject local
settlement. Therefore:

- successful local settlement produces the local final state;
- local transaction failure must be rolled back or represented as an explicit
  recoverable state;
- a PAPER close must not use LIVE provider reconciliation semantics;
- simulated `reconcile()` behavior must not pretend that a provider exists.

## 7. PAPER Position Representation

### 7.1 Decision

For the first PAPER round-trip milestone, Trading Core SHALL retain the existing
legacy `Trade` as the minimal local PAPER position projection.

```text
Trade = current Trading Core-owned PAPER position projection
Trade != universal Position aggregate
```

The first milestone SHALL NOT introduce a universal persistent `Position`
aggregate and SHALL NOT require LIVE broker positions to be persisted into
`Trade`.

### 7.2 Required Meaning

When used for PAPER, `Trade` represents a local exposure projection with:

- one current side;
- a quantity;
- an entry basis;
- an OPEN or CLOSED lifecycle;
- current price and projected unrealized PnL when valued;
- exit price, closed time, and realized PnL after full close;
- a link or deterministic association to the execution effects that created
  and closed it, where needed for idempotency and audit.

The exact additive persistence fields are an implementation decision after this
ADR. They must not turn `Trade` into a broker-authoritative LIVE position.

### 7.3 Position Scope for First Milestone

The first PAPER close scope is one full local exposure represented by one
matching PAPER Trade. The implementation must not imply support for:

- partial close;
- multi-entry allocation beyond the explicitly chosen weighted basis;
- hedged same-symbol positions;
- provider FIFO;
- automatic exits.

### 7.4 Alternatives Rejected

**Dedicated PAPER position model now:** rejected for the first round trip
because it would introduce a second local source of truth and require mapping
between `Trade`, position state, fills, and future outcomes before the current
product needs it.

**Universal Position aggregate:** rejected because it would conflict with
ADR-040's broker authority and add local synchronization semantics to LIVE.

**Owner/provider lookup as position identity:** rejected because it is ambiguous
for multiple accounts and does not identify a local exposure or close target.

This decision can be revisited only when multiple exposures, partial close,
automatic protection, or outcome lineage demonstrate that `Trade` is no longer
an adequate minimal projection.

## 8. Product Close Boundary

### 8.1 Decision

The product SHALL expose one conceptual action:

```text
ClosePosition
```

The application layer SHALL resolve the account's execution mode and delegate
to the correct authority:

```text
ClosePosition
      |
      +--> LIVE  -> PositionCloseCommand -> Broker Service -> provider
      |
      +--> PAPER -> EXIT execution intent -> simulator -> local settlement
```

The frontend must not need to know whether the target is a Kraken txid, a
cTrader position ID, or a local PAPER Trade ID.

### 8.2 Public API Direction

The current endpoint was designed for LIVE broker identity:

```text
POST /api/v1/accounts/{accountId}/positions/close
body: { brokerPositionReference }
```

Future implementation SHOULD evolve this toward a neutral product position
target while preserving the stable product action. A mode-neutral response may
share common status fields, but it must not falsely claim identical convergence
semantics:

- LIVE may remain pending until provider reconciliation confirms exposure state;
- PAPER can reach local CLOSED after the local transaction commits;
- LIVE and PAPER failure/recovery evidence comes from different authorities.

The API change is not implemented by this ADR.

## 9. PAPER Close Semantics

### 9.1 Decision

PAPER full close SHALL be represented as an explicit exit execution intent that
uses the common execution concepts but a local PAPER authority.

```text
OPEN PAPER position
    -> human ClosePosition request
    -> resolve local target and current quantity
    -> create EXIT execution intent
    -> deterministic reducing-exit validation
    -> simulated opposite-side execution
    -> BrokerOrder + Fill
    -> local PAPER settlement
    -> close target Trade and realize PnL
```

The first implementation SHALL support:

```text
FULL_CLOSE_ONLY
NO_PARTIAL_CLOSE
NO_EXPOSURE_REVERSAL
```

### 9.2 Why Not `PositionCloseCommand` for PAPER

`PositionCloseCommand` is retained for LIVE because it assumes:

- broker position identity;
- provider mutation-scope resolution;
- Broker Service execution;
- provider-authoritative reload;
- external reconciliation.

Those assumptions are not true for PAPER. PAPER must not be routed through
`BrokerPositionCloseAdapter`, `BrokerPositionCloseClient`, or Broker Service
mutation capabilities merely to reuse a command name or state machine.

The lifecycle principles of human authorization, ownership, idempotency,
unknown-outcome safety, and explicit audit may be reused conceptually. The
authority and mutation mechanism may not.

### 9.3 Local Target Resolution

Before creating or applying a PAPER EXIT, Trading Core SHALL:

- authenticate the caller;
- verify ownership of the financial account and local position;
- reload the current local position state;
- verify that it is OPEN;
- derive the current side and quantity server-side;
- claim or reserve the target against a competing close;
- reject if the target no longer exists or is already closed.

The frontend must not be authoritative for side, quantity, entry price, or
financial state.

## 10. Entry and Exit Intent Semantics

### 10.1 Explicit Purpose

Side alone is not sufficient to express the meaning of an execution:

```text
SELL may open/increase short exposure or close long exposure
BUY  may open/increase long exposure or close short exposure
```

The execution model SHALL distinguish at least:

```text
ExecutionPurpose = ENTRY | EXIT
```

The exact Java type and persistence shape are left to implementation, but the
semantic distinction is mandatory for a PAPER exit.

### 10.2 EXIT Target

An EXIT intent SHALL identify the local target exposure/Trade and the requested
close quantity. For the first milestone:

- target quantity is the complete current quantity;
- target side is reloaded server-side;
- execution side is derived as its opposite;
- quantity greater than current exposure is rejected;
- an EXIT cannot create a new opposite OPEN Trade.

### 10.3 Lifecycle Reuse Boundary

The current `ExecutionIntent` is entry-oriented and requires TradePlan and risk
approval references. It SHALL NOT be reused unchanged if that forces entry-only
prerequisites onto a PAPER close.

Implementation may either:

- generalize `ExecutionIntent` with explicit purpose and optional exit target;
- introduce an exit-specific execution authorization that reuses the common
  attempt/order/fill/audit mechanisms; or
- use another equivalent design that preserves the invariants of this ADR.

This ADR chooses the semantic requirement, not an implementation class shape.

## 11. Exit Risk Semantics

### 11.1 Common Authority

Risk remains deterministic, explainable, auditable, and fail-closed under ADR-
028. AI cannot approve, override, or alter a PAPER exit.

### 11.2 Reducing Exit Is Not Entry

An exit is a position-reducing action, not a new TradePlan entry. The first
PAPER close SHALL NOT require the existing entry TradePlan/T0 approval as a
prerequisite, consistent with ADR-040's distinction for position reduction.

It SHALL still undergo deterministic safety validation for:

- authenticated ownership;
- PAPER mode and local authority;
- target existence and OPEN state;
- current side and quantity;
- no over-close or reversal;
- current market-data availability for simulation;
- account consistency;
- active close/idempotency conflicts;
- explicit human authorization.

### 11.3 T1-Like Validation

A fresh deterministic reducing-exit validation SHOULD occur immediately before
the simulated financial mutation. It may use an exit-specific validation mode
or an equivalent context, but it must not apply entry-only exposure-increase
rules in a way that prevents a safe full reduction.

If the exit model introduces new Risk Domain rule categories or changes the
meaning of RiskValidationResult, ADR-028/ADR-041 must be amended before
implementation.

### 11.4 Risk Facts Dependency

This ADR does not decide the Account/BrokerAccount identity or PAPER risk-facts
source. A separate decision must define how the Risk Domain receives complete
local PAPER facts. This ADR requires only that the eventual source be trusted,
deterministic, mode-correct, and compatible with fail-closed risk authority.

## 12. Settlement Semantics

### 12.1 Entry

For a successful PAPER ENTRY:

```text
entry Fill
    -> mutate authoritative local balances
    -> open/increase matching local exposure
    -> persist order, fill, Account, and Trade state
```

The current baseline semantics remain:

- BUY debits quote value plus fee and credits base quantity;
- SELL debits base quantity and credits quote value minus fee;
- current simulator fees are zero;
- BUY uses ask and SELL uses bid.

### 12.2 Holding

The authoritative persisted state is quantity, side, entry basis, balances,
and lifecycle. Current price and unrealized PnL SHOULD be dynamically projected
from a timestamped Market Data snapshot rather than persisted on every tick.

For one exposure:

```text
long unrealizedPnL  = (currentPrice - entryPrice) * quantity
short unrealizedPnL = (entryPrice - currentPrice) * quantity
```

Marked equity is a deterministic valuation of local balances and exposure. It
must not be confused with the current baseline implementation, which only
subtracts fees in `PaperSettlementService.recalculateEquity()`.

### 12.3 Exit

For a successful full PAPER EXIT:

```text
exit Fill
    -> reload target OPEN exposure
    -> assert opposite side and full quantity
    -> mutate balances
    -> calculate realized PnL
    -> set exitPrice and closedAt
    -> set target Trade CLOSED
    -> update realized Account state
    -> persist exit order/fill and final local state
```

For a long:

```text
realizedPnL = (exitPrice - entryPrice) * quantity - fees
```

For a short:

```text
realizedPnL = (entryPrice - exitPrice) * quantity - fees
```

The first milestone does not define a new fee policy, multi-lot allocation
policy, or provider-specific accounting behavior.

## 13. Transaction and Atomicity Boundary

### 13.1 Current Concern

The current PAPER acknowledged-result path is not demonstrated as one atomic
local transaction:

```text
ExecuteTradeService                         no transaction annotation
    -> PaperSettlementService                transactional
    -> BrokerOrder persistence                transactional adapter
    -> ExecutionAttempt and Intent persistence
```

Evidence:

- `trading-core/src/main/java/com/hope/trading/trading_core/execution/application/service/ExecuteTradeService.java`
- `trading-core/src/main/java/com/hope/trading/trading_core/execution/application/service/PaperSettlementService.java:31-57`
- `trading-core/src/main/java/com/hope/trading/trading_core/execution/application/pipeline/ExecutionFinalizationStep.java:45-69`
- Story 0038's explicit limitation in `implementation-report.md:29-40`

`PaperSettlementService` also silently returns if the broker account or
financial account is missing, while finalization can continue.

### 13.2 Decision

For PAPER local financial application, the following effects SHALL have one
transaction owner and commit atomically:

```text
BrokerOrder
Fill
Account balance mutation
Trade/position mutation
ExecutionAttempt finalization
ExecutionIntent finalization
settlement-effect idempotency state, if required
```

The current market-data lookup remains outside the database transaction. LIVE
remote broker calls remain outside long database transactions as required by
ADR-041.

### 13.3 Failure

If simulation acknowledges but local settlement fails, the system SHALL NOT
report successful completion while financial state is absent. It must either:

- roll back the local acknowledged-result transaction completely; or
- persist an explicit recoverable state that blocks unsafe retry and identifies
  the recovery action.

For the first deterministic local PAPER implementation, complete local rollback
is preferred where technically possible.

## 14. Idempotency

### 14.1 Shared Principles

The existing Execution Domain principles remain applicable:

- one human action maps to one logical execution;
- idempotency identity is immutable;
- terminal execution cannot be submitted again;
- only one active attempt exists;
- uncertain financial outcomes are not blindly retried.

Evidence: `ADR-029`, `ADR-041`,
`trading-core/src/main/java/com/hope/trading/trading_core/execution/domain/`.

### 14.2 PAPER Entry

PAPER ENTRY reuses the existing `ExecutionIntent` idempotency model. Settlement
must not apply the same intent twice even if finalization is retried.

### 14.3 PAPER Exit

PAPER EXIT SHALL satisfy:

```text
same ClosePosition idempotency key
    -> one exit authorization
    -> one target claim
    -> one financial settlement
```

An already CLOSED target cannot be closed again. A competing exit for the same
target must be rejected deterministically. A separate persistence guard should
be added only if the generalized execution identity cannot itself prove that
the financial effect was applied.

### 14.4 Unknown and Retry

PAPER immediate simulation has no external provider unknown state. Local
transaction failure is not equivalent to a broker timeout. It must follow local
rollback/recovery semantics and must not be mislabeled as LIVE provider
reconciliation.

## 15. Position Query Boundary

The position query service SHALL select its source by execution mode:

```text
PositionQueryService
        |
        +--> LIVE  -> Broker Service/provider projection
        |
        +--> PAPER -> local Account/Trade projection + Market Data valuation
```

The public position DTO SHOULD remain mode-neutral. It may expose an execution
mode or source marker if required for user explanation, but must not require:

- Kraken txids for PAPER;
- cTrader position IDs for PAPER;
- provider mutation scopes;
- Broker Service credentials.

The frontend SHALL request the product action “close this position.” It must
not construct provider-specific mutation mechanics.

## 16. LIVE Compatibility

This decision preserves LIVE behavior:

- Trading Core does not become authoritative for LIVE positions.
- LIVE close remains under ADR-040's position-management capability.
- Broker Service remains responsible for provider translation and reconciliation.
- Kraken FIFO and `reduce_only` remain Broker Service/provider concerns.
- Future cTrader position IDs and hedged/netted semantics remain provider
  concerns.
- LIVE provider references do not leak into PAPER domain semantics.
- PAPER never calls Broker Service mutation or `BrokerPositionCloseClient`.
- No FTMO or prop-firm concept is introduced into the core domain.

## 17. Architectural Invariants

The following invariants are adopted for mode-specific position management:

```text

PAPER_POSITION_AUTHORITY = TRADING_CORE

PAPER_NEVER_CALLS_LIVE_MUTATION_BOUNDARY
PAPER_EXIT_IS_HUMAN_AUTHORIZED
PAPER_EXIT_HAS_EXPLICIT_PURPOSE_AND_LOCAL_TARGET
PAPER_EXIT_CANNOT_INCREASE_EXPOSURE
PAPER_FULL_EXIT_ONLY_FOR_INITIAL_ROUND_TRIP
PAPER_FULL_EXIT_IS_IDEMPOTENT

```

## 18. Scope Boundary

This ADR does NOT decide or implement:

- Account/BrokerAccount identity or migration;
- PAPER Risk Facts source/configuration;
- changes to risk rule definitions;
- a universal Position aggregate;
- partial close;
- SL/TP modification or automatic triggering;
- trailing stops;
- fees, slippage, latency, liquidity, or partial fills;
- pending-order simulation;
- cTrader implementation;
- FTMO or prop-firm integration;
- backtesting or historical replay;
- TradeOutcome strategy analytics;
- event sourcing;
- WebSocket/SSE position streaming;
- redesign of LIVE close behavior.

## 19. Consequences

### Positive

- Position authority is explicit for both execution modes.
- PAPER can remain independent of external credentials and provider state.
- LIVE broker authority and reconciliation remain intact.
- PAPER exit can reuse common execution, order, fill, audit, and idempotency
  concepts without reusing provider-specific close commands.
- A single product-level close action remains possible without misleading the
  frontend about provider mechanics.
- Full-close/no-reversal semantics protect the first PAPER round trip.
- The first PAPER model remains small by retaining `Trade` rather than adding a
  universal Position aggregate.
- Future cTrader compatibility is preserved by keeping provider mechanics below
  Broker Service.

### Accepted Costs

- A mode-aware product close boundary is required.
- Execution authorization must distinguish ENTRY and EXIT.
- Local PAPER position targeting and settlement require additional persistence
  invariants.
- PAPER and LIVE have different completion and reconciliation semantics.
- A separate Account identity/risk-facts decision is required before safe
  implementation.
- Existing transaction and close-path debt remains visible rather than hidden.

## 20. Alternatives Considered

### A. Reuse `PositionCloseCommand` for PAPER

Rejected. It would import broker identity, mutation scope, external
reconciliation, and Broker Service authority into a Trading Core-owned local
simulation.

### B. Use a bare opposite-side execution with no explicit purpose

Rejected. BUY/SELL side does not distinguish opening exposure from closing it.
Without an explicit purpose and target, settlement can create an opposite OPEN
Trade instead of closing the selected position.

### C. Create a dedicated PAPER close command that duplicates execution

Rejected for the first milestone. It would duplicate idempotency, attempts,
orders, fills, audit, and human authorization unless it eventually delegates
back to the common execution model.

### D. Use a shared `ClosePosition` boundary with mode-specific implementations

Accepted. It preserves a coherent product concept while keeping authority and
mutation mechanics separate.

### E. Introduce a universal persistent Position aggregate

Rejected for now. LIVE remains broker-authoritative and the repository does not
show a current requirement for a universal local position identity.

### F. Use owner/provider lookup as the PAPER position target

Rejected. It is ambiguous for multiple accounts and cannot identify one local
exposure safely.

### G. Require full entry T0 risk evaluation for every exit

Rejected. A reducing exit is not an entry and entry-only exposure-increase
rules must not make safe reduction impossible. Deterministic reducing-exit
validation remains mandatory.

### H. Add simulation realism before position lifecycle

Rejected. Fees, slippage, partial fills, and latency do not make an entry-only
PAPER path a usable validation environment.

## 21. Implementation Impact

## 22. Testing Requirements for Future Implementation

The implementation must prove, not merely assume:

### PAPER entry

```text
application-created PAPER account
    -> real local identity/risk setup
    -> real deterministic T0/T1
    -> simulated entry
    -> persisted open Trade
    -> reload
```

### PAPER position query

```text
PAPER -> local Trading Core source
LIVE  -> Broker Service source
```

PAPER queries must produce zero Broker Service mutation calls.

### PAPER exit

```text
open local PAPER Trade
    -> human ClosePosition
    -> EXIT target/purpose
    -> reducing validation
    -> simulated opposite fill
    -> realized PnL
    -> CLOSED Trade
    -> persisted Account/order/fill state
```

Test both long-to-short-side-exit and short-to-long-side-exit, without allowing
the exit to create opposite exposure.

### Idempotency

- same entry request produces one financial mutation;
- same exit request produces one exit intent and one settlement;
- already closed target rejects a second close;
- competing active exit is rejected;
- terminal intent cannot settle twice;
- transaction failure does not duplicate balances or Trade transitions.

### Failure atomicity

Inject failures after fill construction and during local settlement/final
persistence. Assert complete rollback or an explicit recoverable state; never a
successful terminal state with missing financial mutation.

### Restart

```text
entry -> reload -> query local position -> close
      -> reload -> assert CLOSED + realized Account state
```

### LIVE regression

Run Story 0033's existing LIVE position-close tests and verify that the new
PAPER branch does not call Broker Service or change provider reconciliation.

## 23. ADR Boundaries and Follow-up Decisions

### Separate ADR required: Account Identity and PAPER Risk Facts

A separate ADR is required before implementing the first PAPER risk/identity
story. It must decide:

- whether Account and BrokerAccount retain distinct IDs;
- the canonical explicit relation between them;
- multiple-account and uniqueness semantics;
- how PAPER risk configuration is initialized and assigned;
- the mode-aware source of T0/T1 facts;
- whether `BrokerRiskFactsPort` remains the public port name or is generalized;
- migration/backfill behavior for existing LIVE accounts.

This is broader than position close and is intentionally not hidden in ADR-042.

### Possible ADR-028/041 Amendment

Amend ADR-028 or ADR-041 only if reducing-exit validation changes the Risk
Domain contract, introduces new rule categories, or changes fail-closed
semantics. A new evaluation context using existing risk authority can remain a
dependency of this ADR without a separate risk ADR.

### No ADR required for mechanical work

No new ADR is required merely for:

- retaining `Trade` as the first local PAPER projection;
- using current Market Data for deterministic valuation;
- writing persistence tests;
- keeping provider mechanics in Broker Service;
- adding neutral API fields after this boundary is accepted.

## 24. Migration and Rollout Constraints

- This ADR introduces no schema migration.
- Future migrations must be additive and explicitly backfillable.
- No existing LIVE position data may be reinterpreted as local PAPER state.
- No existing provider position reference may be stored as a PAPER mutation
  target.
- Existing Story 0038 fill migration `V11__persist_execution_broker_fills.sql`
  remains valid and is not renamed by this ADR.
- A future implementation must not silently change Account uniqueness semantics
  while solving identity.

## 25. Deferred Work

The following remain explicitly deferred:

- partial close;
- SL/TP modification and automatic exits;
- trailing stops;
- fees, slippage, latency, partial fills, liquidity, and pending orders;
- backtesting and historical replay;
- strategy validation and parameter sweeps;
- TradeOutcome strategy analytics;
- automatic background reconciliation for PAPER;
- universal Position aggregate;
- cTrader and FTMO integration;
- provider-specific semantics in Trading Core;
- event sourcing and tick-level financial persistence.

## 26. Open Questions

These questions belong to the separate Account Identity/Risk Facts decision or
future refinement:

- Should the first local PAPER model support one net exposure per symbol only?
- Should a neutral ClosePosition response expose a distinct local completion
  status or normalize it with LIVE statuses?
- Should exit authorization be an extended `ExecutionIntent` or a dedicated
  exit command sharing execution effects?
- Should local PAPER current valuations be persisted as snapshots for audit, or
  remain dynamic projections with a timestamped source?
- Is a separate accepted PAPER ADR useful after the identity/risk ADR, or are
  these mode decisions sufficient?

## 27. Consequences for Story Refinement

No Engineering Story is created by this ADR. Once ADR-042 and the separate
Account Identity/Risk Facts ADR are accepted and their open questions are
resolved, the first implementation Story should remain:

> Establish PAPER Account Identity and Risk Facts.

The subsequent sequence should be:

```text
Account Identity and PAPER Risk Facts
    -> local PAPER Position Query and Valuation
    -> PAPER Full Exit through EXIT ExecutionIntent
    -> persisted PAPER Round-Trip Regression
```

The sequence must not begin with simulation realism or cTrader integration.
