# ADR-052 - PAPER Cash Realized State and Equity Valuation Authority

## Status

Accepted for Story 0086 implementation.

## Context

Story 0085 introduced cash-settled PAPER derivatives, but persisted
`Account.equity` was used both for realized settlement effects and for the
current account value. That representation cannot safely support repeated
mark-to-market valuation.

## Decision

For PAPER CASH accounts, Trading Core persists an explicit `realizedBalance`.
It is the authoritative settlement baseline and excludes unrealized P&L.

```text
equity = realizedBalance + aggregate unrealized P&L of OPEN derivative Trades
```

`Account.equity` is the latest successfully computed valuation snapshot. It is
never incremented by the current unrealized P&L.

Valuation uses the provider-neutral Market Data snapshot contract. V1 uses the
midpoint of fresh bid and ask prices. Risk facts are assembled only after a
successful PAPER CASH valuation refresh; stale or unavailable data fails closed.

## Authority Boundaries

- PAPER CASH: Trading Core realized baseline and valuation are authoritative.
- PAPER INVENTORY: existing balance and settlement behavior remains
  authoritative and is not migrated to CASH semantics.
- LIVE accounts: broker synchronization remains authoritative; local PAPER
  valuation is not applied.

`peakEquity` is advanced only by a successful fresh valuation.

## Migration

Existing PAPER CASH accounts receive `realizedBalance = equity`, because Story
0085 had no mark-to-market behavior. Existing PAPER INVENTORY, LIVE, and
UNKNOWN accounts are not assigned a PAPER CASH baseline.

## Consequences

- Settlement updates `realizedBalance` and recomputes `equity` from open trade
  marks.
- Closed trades contribute no unrealized P&L.
- Valuation freshness is explicit and is not inferred from a persisted price.
- Continuous background valuation, funding, leverage, margin, liquidation, and
  valuation history remain out of scope.
