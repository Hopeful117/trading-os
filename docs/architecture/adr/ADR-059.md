# ADR-059 - Objective Future Market Outcomes and Evaluation Truth

## Status

Accepted for Story 0107C implementation.

## Related Decisions

- ADR-035 - Strategy Evaluation Context and Strategy-Agnostic Evaluation Pipeline.
- ADR-048 - Intelligence Evidence and Authority.
- ADR-056 - Deterministic Market Intelligence Evidence and Analyzer Boundary.
- ADR-057 - Historical Market Evidence, Known-At Semantics, and Source Retention.
- ADR-058 - Historical Market Intelligence Replay and Evidence Acquisition Boundary.

## Decision

Trading OS represents objective future market evolution as a separate,
provider-neutral, immutable Market Intelligence outcome:

```text
captured reference evidence <= T0
        +
captured bounded OHLC evidence (T0, T0 + H]
        -> ObservedMarketOutcome
```

An objective outcome is not an ML label, prediction, strategy result, trade
result, P&L calculation, Risk result, or execution result.

Market Data owns historical facts, evidence eligibility, correction selection,
coverage, gaps, and evidence fingerprints. Market Intelligence owns reference
semantics, maturity, completeness interpretation, objective metrics, outcome
definition, and immutable outcome provenance.

## Temporal Integrity

The T0 analysis context contains only evidence available at or before T0. Future
outcome evidence is acquired after the analysis boundary and must never enter:

- `IntelligenceContext @ T0`;
- its fingerprint;
- deterministic analysis inputs;
- feature projections derived from that context.

The reference price is the close of the latest eligible captured, closed,
non-synthetic observation at or before T0. The future path uses only the bounded
Market Data range contract with `CAPTURED` evidence, `FIFTEEN_MINUTES`,
`fromExclusive = T0`, and `toInclusive = T0 + H`.

## V1 Outcome Semantics

The outcome definition version is:

```text
market-outcome-v1
```

The version owns reference semantics, future interval, range boundaries,
evidence policy, completeness requirements, final-price selection, formulas,
and rounding semantics.

The outcome is mature when:

```text
now >= T0 + H
```

Before maturity the result is `PENDING` and has no finalized metrics. Matured
results use the following statuses:

- `AVAILABLE`: reference evidence exists and the future range is complete.
- `INCOMPLETE`: the future window matured but coverage or gaps prevent a
  trustworthy complete result.
- `UNAVAILABLE`: reference or usable future evidence is unavailable.

Missing evidence is never substituted with zero.

For a complete range, all authoritative calculations use `BigDecimal`:

```text
finalPrice = final selected future candle close
futureHigh = maximum selected future high
futureLow = minimum selected future low
forwardReturn = (finalPrice - referencePrice) / referencePrice
futureHighReturn = (futureHigh - referencePrice) / referencePrice
futureLowReturn = (futureLow - referencePrice) / referencePrice
futureRange = (futureHigh - futureLow) / referencePrice
```

Returns are signed decimal fractions. They are not MFE, MAE, volatility, P&L,
or direction-dependent excursion values.

## Immutability and Identity

Outcomes are immutable research artifacts. A later captured correction that
changes selected future evidence creates a new outcome version; it never silently
overwrites the old artifact.

The deterministic outcome fingerprint includes stable semantic material:

```text
definition version
market
T0
horizon
reference evidence fingerprint
future range evidence fingerprint
status
calculated values
```

It excludes `computedAt`, database IDs, random UUIDs, and execution timing.
Identical semantic evidence is idempotent and must not create duplicate rows.

`AnalysisExecution` may be retained as an optional reference, but it is not the
fundamental outcome identity. An objective outcome may be shared by multiple
analyses.

## Boundaries and Non-Goals

The outcome service must not call current providers, repair gaps, schedule work,
create ML labels, evaluate strategies, simulate trades, calculate account P&L,
authorize Risk, submit execution, or introduce a new microservice.

An internal controlled invocation may be exposed under `/internal/**`; no
Gateway, frontend, or public route is introduced.

## Consequences

The design preserves temporal honesty and makes objective market evolution
replayable and suitable for later research and dataset construction. It requires
versioned provenance, explicit missingness, immutable persistence, and separate
future evidence acquisition from strict historical replay.
