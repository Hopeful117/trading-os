# ADR-070 - Trade Outcome Association with PAPER Trade Lifecycle

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted |
| **Date** | 2026-10-22 |
| **Related ADRs** | ADR-029, ADR-043, ADR-047 |
| **Related Stories** | Story 0036, Story 0061, Post-trade Decision History V1 |

---

## 1. Context

Trading Core persists an entry-oriented `TradeOutcome` snapshot for an accepted
execution. PAPER settlement separately creates the authoritative local `Trade`
that represents the PAPER position lifecycle and realized trade facts.

The existing model has no durable association between those records. The PAPER
exit path already links its EXIT `ExecutionIntent` to the authoritative `Trade`
through `targetTradeId`, but history cannot safely traverse from the entry
`TradeOutcome` to that `Trade` without an explicit relationship.

Broker identifiers must not be used for this association. Broker position
references and broker order identifiers have different semantics and may not
represent the same lifecycle or close scope.

---

## 2. Decision

`trade_outcome` SHALL contain a nullable `trade_id` association to the local
`trades` table:

```text
trade_outcome.trade_id -> trades.trade_id
```

The association represents the authoritative PAPER `Trade` created from the
entry execution represented by the `TradeOutcome`.

`PaperSettlementService` SHALL persist and return the created `Trade` identity
for PAPER entry settlement, and the corresponding entry `TradeOutcome` SHALL
store that identity.

The association is nullable because LIVE execution and other outcomes may not
have a local authoritative PAPER `Trade`. A null value means the association is
unavailable; it must never be interpreted as zero P&L, a successful close, or an
inferred position.

Once assigned, `trade_id` is immutable.

`Trade` remains authoritative for PAPER position lifecycle and realized trade
facts. `TradeOutcome` remains an entry/outcome snapshot and does not become a
second position or trade ledger.

The existing EXIT `ExecutionIntent.targetTradeId` remains the authoritative link
from a PAPER close execution to the `Trade` being closed.

Decision History may traverse these relationships but must not duplicate
settlement, close, or P&L business logic.

---

## 3. Migration and Backfill

The schema change is additive and nullable. Existing rows remain null unless a
deterministic authoritative backfill can be proven from persisted relationships.
No heuristic matching by instrument, timestamps, price, quantity, broker order,
or broker position reference is permitted.

If a deterministic backfill cannot be proven, the association remains
unavailable and is exposed explicitly by read models.

---

## 4. Consequences

Positive:

- PAPER Decision History can traverse entry execution, local position, exit
  execution, close, and realized trade facts without inference.
- LIVE history can truthfully expose unavailable local position lineage.
- Existing execution, settlement, close, and Risk authorities remain unchanged.
- The association survives reload and process restart through normal persistence.

Trade-offs:

- PAPER settlement must return the created Trade identity to its caller.
- The entry outcome persistence contract gains a nullable field and migration.
- Historical rows may remain partially linked when deterministic backfill is not
  possible.

---

## 5. Rejected Alternatives

- Matching `TradeOutcome` to `Trade` by instrument, quantity, price, or time.
- Matching broker order identifiers to broker position references.
- Making `TradeOutcome` the new position or settlement ledger.
- Reconstructing the relationship in Angular or a history DTO mapper.
- Treating missing `trade_id` as a completed trade with zero result.

---

## 6. Implementation Boundary

This ADR authorizes the smallest persistence and settlement changes required to
populate the association, plus a read-only Decision History projection that
consumes authoritative existing facts. It does not authorize changes to Risk,
execution state transitions, broker close semantics, S1, research, or LIVE
execution behavior.
