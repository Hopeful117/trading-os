# ADR-050 - Account-Scoped Opportunity Provenance

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-10

## Related ADRs

- ADR-014 - Trading Decision Pipeline
- ADR-043 - Account Identity and Mode-Specific Risk Facts
- ADR-044 - Service-to-Service Trust and Actor Propagation
- ADR-047 - Manual Trade Plans as a First-Class Trade Plan Origin

## Decision

An Opportunity produced by an account-scoped scan is permanently associated
with the financial `accountId` used by that scan. The association is analytical
provenance, not execution authorization.

An account-scoped Opportunity must retain `accountId`, `sourceScanId`,
`sourceScanMarketId`, `analysisExecutionId`, and `marketId`. The relationship is
immutable for every Opportunity version. A new account or scan creates a
distinct Opportunity lineage rather than reusing an existing lineage from
another account.

Trade Plan preparation must verify that its account matches every referenced
Opportunity account. Execution remains subject to deterministic Risk and
human-approval boundaries.

Manual and non-account-scoped Opportunities may remain without scan context,
but they cannot be used to satisfy an account-scoped Opportunity workflow.

Historical rows created before this decision may have incomplete provenance.
They remain readable for audit, but must be rejected for account-scoped Trade
Plan preparation until their account provenance is known.
