## 1. Context

Trading OS is an AI-assisted discretionary trading platform. Its product value
comes from reducing the trader's decision burden while preserving capital,
deterministic controls, explicit human authority, and explainable history.

The platform combines several kinds of information and decision:

- provider and platform market facts;
- deterministic derived market evidence;
- statistical or machine-learning evidence;
- agent and LLM interpretation;
- human discretionary decisions;
- versioned strategy evaluation;
- trader-facing opportunities and Trade Plans;
- deterministic account-specific Risk authorization;
- human-authorized financial execution.

Existing ADRs define most individual boundaries. They do not yet provide one
cross-cutting authority model that explains how these outputs relate, which
outputs may override other outputs, or how future ML and agents must remain
non-authoritative for deterministic controls.

Without that model, the following unsafe interpretations could emerge:

- a favorable deterministic assessment could be treated as an opportunity
  without a StrategyEvaluation and StrategyMatch;
- an ML score could be treated as a stronger version of deterministic evidence;
- several agent recommendations could be treated as a consensus decision;
- a TradePlan could be treated as execution authorization;
- Risk approval could be confused with human willingness to execute;
- a human-facing UI label could blur analytical attractiveness with financial
  authorization;
- historical assessments could be rewritten after new candles, model results,
  or outcomes become available.

The accepted first deterministic capability, Trend Context Assessment, exposes
the need for this decision. Its factual result may be:

```text
direction = UP
regime = TRENDING
phase = PULLBACK
attention = CONTEXTUALLY_ATTRACTIVE
```

That result means that a deterministic market context is worth human or
strategy review. It does not mean `BUY`, a StrategyMatch, Risk approval, or an
execution command.

Trading OS must also remain able to conclude:

```text
UNKNOWN
NO_SETUP
WATCH
```

without manufacturing a trade candidate. Absence of a trade is a valid product
outcome.

## 2. Decision Question

How does Trading OS distinguish authoritative source facts, deterministic
derived evidence, statistical/ML evidence, agent/LLM interpretation, human
discretionary decisions, deterministic financial authorization, and execution
authority while preserving the existing domain boundaries?

## 3. Decision

Trading OS adopts two related but separate authority models.

### 3.1 Evidence and interpretation authority

The conceptual evidence hierarchy is:

```text
Authoritative source facts
        ↓
Deterministic derived evidence
        ↓
Statistical / ML evidence
        ↓
Agent / LLM interpretation
        ↓
Human discretionary interpretation
```

This hierarchy describes epistemic and analytical authority. It is not
necessarily a synchronous runtime call chain.

A lower-authority interpretive layer may consume, explain, challenge, or enrich
higher-authority evidence. It must not mutate, redefine, or silently replace
that evidence.

The terms have these meanings:

| Term | Meaning | Authority boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source fact | A fact supplied by the service that owns and normalizes the source contract | The owning service is authoritative for that fact within its contract |
| Derived fact/evidence | A reproducible result calculated from identified source inputs and versioned rules | The deterministic producer is authoritative for the result of its rule, not for the source facts |
| Statistical evidence | A model-derived estimate, similarity, ranking, anomaly, or calibration result | The model is authoritative only for its versioned output and uncertainty, not for deterministic facts or authorization |
| Interpretation/proposal | A contextual explanation, challenge, hypothesis, or recommendation | The producer owns the proposal; it does not become a source or deterministic fact |
| Human decision | A discretionary acceptance, rejection, deferral, refinement, or execution authorization | The human owns the discretionary action, but cannot bypass deterministic system constraints through the ordinary workflow |

The word `truth` is not used as a substitute for provenance. Authority means
which component owns the contract and can establish the meaning of its output.

### 3.2 Financial and execution authority

The separate financial chain is:

```text
Market/context evidence
        ↓
StrategyEvaluation
        ↓
StrategyMatch
        ↓
TradingOpportunity
        ↓
Human-reviewed TradePlan
        ↓
Deterministic Risk Engine
        ├── REJECTED / UNAVAILABLE → STOP
        └── APPROVED
                ↓
      explicit human execution authorization
                ↓
             Execution
```

The chain describes increasing business semantics and authority. Analytical
confidence never becomes financial or execution authority by implication.

The accepted Trend Context example therefore has this relationship:

```text
Market Data
    ↓
TrendContextAssessment
    ↓
IntelligenceObservation
    ↓
StrategyEvaluation
    ↓
StrategyMatch
    ↓
TradingOpportunity
    ↓
Human review / TradePlan
    ↓
Risk
    ↓
Human execution authorization
    ↓
Execution
```

A favorable `TrendContextAssessment` MUST NOT directly create:

- a `StrategyMatch`;
- a `TradingOpportunity`;
- a `TradePlan`;
- Risk approval;
- an `ExecutionIntent` or execution command.

The StrategyEvaluation and StrategyMatch boundary from ADR-034 remains
mandatory.

## 4. Product and Capital-Preservation Principle

The authority model supports the following product priority:

```text
1. Preserve capital
2. Avoid mediocre trades
3. Control risk
4. Exploit good trends
5. Seek performance
```

Market Intelligence and future probabilistic capabilities must optimize for
honest selectivity, not candidate volume. A zero-result scan, `NO_SETUP`,
`UNKNOWN`, `WATCH`, stale context, or explicit rejection is a successful and
valid workflow outcome when the evidence does not justify further action.

No authority layer is required to produce a direction when required evidence is
missing, stale, contradictory, or unsafe.

## 5. Domain Ownership

### 5.1 Market Data

Market Data owns normalized public market facts and their source/freshness
metadata within its contracts, including as applicable:

- market identity and catalogue data;
- provider-normalized prices and OHLC;
- market state and tradability;
- instrument metadata and market constraints;
- source timestamps and freshness information.

Broker Service may report provider/account technical facts through
broker-neutral contracts, but it does not replace Market Data ownership of
instrument metadata or market constraints, consistent with ADR-045.

Market Data does not decide whether a market is attractive, whether a strategy
matches, whether a TradePlan is financially authorized, or whether an order may
be executed.

### 5.2 Deterministic Market Intelligence

Deterministic Market Intelligence consumes identified authoritative inputs and
produces reproducible, explainable, versioned derived evidence.

Examples include:

- structural observations;
- Trend Context Assessment;
- deterministic freshness and completeness findings;
- deterministic hard exclusions;
- normalized analytical measurements;
- future deterministic market capabilities.

Its output is authoritative for the result of the declared deterministic rule,
not for source facts owned by another service and not for account-specific
financial authorization.

The same accepted inputs, assessment cut-off, configuration, and rule versions
must produce the same deterministic output. A deterministic component must
fail explicitly when it cannot calculate safely. It must not replace missing
evidence with a neutral value, model confidence, or an optimistic direction.

### 5.3 Market Intelligence orchestration

Market Intelligence owns analysis context construction, capability selection,
execution orchestration, consolidation, and promotion of eligible results into
durable business observations, consistent with ADR-020 and ADR-021.

Orchestration does not grant equal authority to every context section or result.
The orchestrator must preserve the source, producer type, authority category,
freshness, and provenance of each contribution.

Market Intelligence must not assemble authoritative financial Risk snapshots,
replace the Risk Domain, or execute broker commands.

### 5.4 IntelligenceContext

`IntelligenceContext` is the analysis context/input-assembly concept defined by
ADR-020 and ADR-021. It is not an unstructured source of authority and is not a
universal recommendation object.

Each context section must remain attributable to its owning source or producer
and should expose, where applicable:

- classification and authorized consumer;
- source identity;
- occurrence and fetch timestamps;
- freshness/validity;
- available, stale, missing, or unavailable state;
- deterministic, statistical, contextual, account, or user-context category.

Selecting a section for an analysis does not change its authority. An AI Engine
or agent receives a policy-selected context view, not unrestricted authority to
query source services.

### 5.5 IntelligenceObservation and evidence

`IntelligenceObservation` is durable, reusable analytical business evidence. It
preserves the meaning and lineage of an observation without becoming a TradePlan
or authorization result.

ADR-025 remains authoritative for the observation model:

- observations are immutable in meaning;
- evolution creates a new observation;
- observations require evidence;
- evidence is traceable to capability results, artifacts, and source data;
- validity and supersession are explicit;
- AI is read-only with respect to deterministic observations.

This ADR clarifies that a typed `TrendContextAssessment` may be carried as the
payload of an `IntelligenceObservation` without replacing the generic
observation lineage model. A deterministic assessment may include factual
fields, attention outcomes, exclusions, and provenance. None of those fields
constitutes a StrategyMatch or authorization.

AI or agent results may be retained as separate technical results or promoted
to an explicitly typed observation when the existing Market Intelligence
promotion rules are satisfied. Promotion does not allow an AI result to mutate
or overwrite deterministic evidence.

### 5.6 StrategyDefinition

`StrategyDefinition` owns versioned strategy criteria, as established by
ADR-034. It may define applicable markets, timeframes, required observations,
entry conditions, direction rules, invalidation conditions, parameters, and
validation metadata.

A reusable market observation must not silently embed the complete trading
strategy. A deterministic analytical profile may control how evidence is
calculated without becoming a complete `StrategyDefinition`.

Strategy does not own:

- Market Data acquisition;
- scanner orchestration;
- account or Risk authorization;
- position sizing authority;
- broker execution;
- human execution authorization;
- AI authority.

### 5.7 StrategyEvaluation

`StrategyEvaluation` is one evaluation of one versioned `StrategyDefinition`
against a specific context. It may match or not match.

The evaluator is the deterministic authority for whether that strategy version's
declared conditions were satisfied. It must retain the strategy identity,
version, context references, condition results, and relevant provenance.

Strategy evaluation must not be inferred merely because a generic observation
has an attractive attention outcome.

### 5.8 StrategyMatch

`StrategyMatch` is the immutable fact that a specific strategy version satisfied
its conditions for a specific market and context at a specific time.

It is stronger than a generic observation but weaker than Risk authorization.
It does not authorize a TradePlan or execution. It must remain persisted and
traceable according to ADR-034.

### 5.9 TradingOpportunity

`TradingOpportunity` is a trader-facing candidate derived from a valid upstream
`StrategyMatch`, not directly from a generic Observation or a favorable
TrendContextAssessment.

For the current V1 boundary, one opportunity derives from one StrategyMatch as
specified by ADR-034. An opportunity is not:

- Risk approval;
- a TradePlan;
- an execution command;
- a guarantee of profitability.

The opportunity may explain its source and validity, but it must not imply
authority that its upstream evidence does not possess.

### 5.10 TradePlan

`TradePlan` is a concrete human-reviewable trading proposal. It separates
trading rationale from execution parameters and remains distinct from both an
opportunity and an execution command.

ADR-027 and ADR-047 remain authoritative:

- an opportunity-origin plan references its upstream opportunity;
- a manual-origin plan may be authored by the human without a synthetic
  opportunity;
- both origins use the same Risk and execution path;
- a TradePlan is not execution authorization;
- a changed proposal creates a new version or plan according to the existing
  lifecycle;
- human rationale does not override deterministic Risk rules.

Market Intelligence may produce or support a plan, but the plan does not become
authorized because its rationale cites attractive deterministic, statistical,
or agent evidence.

### 5.11 RiskEvaluation and Risk Engine

The Risk Engine is the deterministic final authority for account-specific
financial authorization, as established by ADR-028.

Trading Core assembles the authoritative immutable Risk Evaluation Context.
Risk evaluates the exact TradePlan against applicable account, portfolio,
market, broker, and policy facts. Risk owns the authorization result for the
rules it owns:

```text
APPROVED
APPROVED_WITH_WARNINGS
REJECTED
```

Missing required Risk information fails closed. The Risk Engine does not depend
on ML or LLM availability for core deterministic authorization.

Neither Trend Context, StrategyMatch, TradingOpportunity, ML, an agent, an LLM,
the UI, nor human enthusiasm may convert a Risk rejection or unavailable result
into approval.

### 5.12 Human discretionary authority

The human trader may:

- inspect source facts and evidence;
- accept, reject, or defer a context or thesis;
- decline an attractive setup;
- create or refine a TradePlan within the established workflow;
- choose not to trade;
- explicitly authorize execution after the required Risk approval.

The human does not become the owner of source facts, deterministic calculations,
Risk rules, or execution state merely by disagreeing with them. An ordinary UI
action must not bypass deterministic constraints. In particular, `Risk =
REJECTED` remains a stop condition.

Any exceptional administrative override would require a separate explicit
governance decision and is not created by this ADR.

### 5.13 Execution

The Execution Domain begins only after the established Risk and human gates.
ADR-029 and ADR-041 remain authoritative for `ExecutionIntent`, idempotency,
execution attempts, reconciliation, human authorization, and safe financial
side effects.

Execution consumes an approved TradePlan through the established execution
contract. It must not consume an ML score, agent recommendation, UI confidence,
or Trend Context outcome as execution authority.

Broker Service remains a technical, broker-neutral adapter. It does not
authorize trades, evaluate strategy, or decide recovery/business state.

## 6. Deterministic Evidence Contract

Every material deterministic conclusion must be reconstructable from:

- immutable input or source references;
- market/scope identity;
- assessment timestamp and cut-off;
- relevant evidence window;
- profile/configuration ID and version;
- rule ID and version;
- source freshness and completeness;
- deterministic findings and exclusions;
- producer and contract version;
- immutable lineage or content fingerprint where applicable.

The result must distinguish at least the meaning required by its domain. Safe
states may include:

```text
UNKNOWN
NO_SETUP
INSUFFICIENT
STALE
INVALID
```

These values are not required to be one platform-wide enum. Their semantics
must not be collapsed into a neutral direction or successful analysis.

For the accepted Trend Context capability, the dedicated input contract and
versioned rule catalogue define the retained V1 calculations. The formulas and
profile thresholds remain in that domain specification and tests, not in this
cross-cutting ADR.

## 7. Immutability, Versioning, and Lineage

Historical evidence is append-oriented and immutable in meaning.

When any of the following changes:

- source data or source interpretation;
- newly available candle or market fact;
- profile or configuration;
- deterministic rule;
- strategy version;
- ML model or feature schema;
- agent context or interpretation;

the system creates a new assessment, evidence contribution, observation,
evaluation, or result version as appropriate. It does not rewrite what the
system previously knew at the earlier decision time.

Supersession and expiry are explicit relationships/statuses. A newer result may
supersede an older one for current consumption, but historical consumers must
still be able to retrieve the earlier result.

The minimum lineage should preserve, where relevant:

```text
source snapshot/reference
    → AnalysisExecution
    → deterministic assessment / Observation
    → StrategyEvaluation
    → StrategyMatch
    → TradingOpportunity
    → TradePlan/version
    → RiskEvaluation (T0/T1 where applicable)
    → human review / ExecutionIntent
    → ExecutionAttempt / provider outcome
    → position / exit / outcome
```

The architecture does not require duplicating complete raw datasets when an
immutable source reference, digest, and replayable source contract are
sufficient. Each owning service retains responsibility for its source data and
retention policy.

## 8. Temporal Integrity and No Look-Ahead

An analytical snapshot may contain only information available at its assessment
cut-off. This invariant applies to:

- deterministic assessments;
- `IntelligenceObservation` evidence;
- StrategyEvaluation and StrategyMatch inputs;
- future ML feature snapshots;
- agent ContextPacks;
- historical learning datasets.

Every material input must be at or before the applicable cut-off, or explicitly
classified as a later outcome/label rather than a feature.

Future information may be attached after the intended observation horizon as an
outcome, label, or subsequent assessment. It must not mutate the original
feature/evidence snapshot.

This preserves reproducibility, honest PAPER evaluation, backtesting integrity,
future ML training integrity, and protection against data leakage.

Late analysis results follow ADR-021: they may be retained for audit or
comparison, but they must not silently modify an expired, cancelled, or already
consolidated historical result.

## 9. Statistical and ML Boundary

This ADR does not define an ML algorithm, model registry, feature store, or
training process. It defines the future authority boundary.

ML may produce separate statistical evidence such as:

- historical calibration;
- similarity to prior contexts;
- anomaly or out-of-distribution evidence;
- ranking among already eligible contexts;
- setup-quality evidence with explicit event/horizon semantics;
- trader-specific patterns;
- abstention or insufficient-data results.

Where relevant, every ML result should preserve conceptually:

- model identity and version;
- feature/input snapshot reference and schema version;
- training dataset and evaluation provenance;
- label definition and horizon where applicable;
- inference timestamp and validity;
- uncertainty, calibration, drift, and abstention metadata;
- correlation to the deterministic assessment.

ML MUST NOT:

- rewrite authoritative source facts;
- rewrite deterministic evidence or its rule result;
- turn invalid, stale, or incomplete deterministic evidence into valid evidence;
- remove deterministic hard exclusions;
- silently promote or enable a strategy;
- authorize a TradePlan or Risk result;
- authorize or submit execution.

ML output is evidence, not authorization. This ADR does not require a generic
`winProbability`; any future probability must identify its exact event, horizon,
calibration, and uncertainty.

## 10. Agent and LLM Boundary

This ADR accepts the future multi-agent direction without defining an agent
platform.

Agents may consume a targeted, policy-selected context containing, where
authorized:

- deterministic evidence;
- ML/statistical evidence;
- portfolio or account context;
- strategy context;
- news or macro context;
- original thesis and TradePlan;
- historical evidence references.

Market Intelligence remains the business orchestrator. Agents receive selected
context and do not obtain unrestricted service, database, credential, or broker
access.

Agents may:

- summarize structured evidence;
- reason across heterogeneous context;
- expose contradictions;
- challenge a thesis;
- identify missing information;
- compare alternative interpretations;
- propose a question, review action, or trade context for human consideration.

Agents MUST NOT:

- rewrite authoritative source facts;
- redefine deterministic structural conclusions as authoritative facts;
- remove deterministic exclusions;
- override Risk;
- create execution authority;
- submit, cancel, modify, or close financial commands autonomously under the
  current architecture.

### 10.1 Structured agent results

When an agent result participates in a system workflow, it should not exist as
arbitrary prose only. A future structured proposal should be able to preserve
conceptually:

```text
proposal or conclusion
supporting evidence references
contradicting evidence references
assumptions
missing information
uncertainty
source provenance
context timestamp and validity
agent role/model/version
human-review status
```

The final schema, provider contract, prompt policy, and ContextPack design are
future decisions. This ADR only requires that material claims remain visibly
interpretive and traceable.

### 10.2 Multi-agent disagreement

Multiple agents do not create authority through consensus:

```text
Market Agent      says attractive
Macro Agent       says acceptable
Portfolio Agent   says manageable
Trade Review      says proceed
```

does not override:

```text
Risk = REJECTED
```

Agent agreement remains interpretative evidence. Agents may disagree because of
different sources, timestamps, assumptions, or context. The system preserves
the disagreement and its evidence rather than averaging it into deterministic
fact or financial authorization.

## 11. Human, Risk, and Execution Gates

The authority chain has two distinct final boundaries:

1. **Risk authority:** the Risk Engine decides whether the exact TradePlan is
   financially authorized under current deterministic account/risk facts.
2. **Human execution authority:** the human explicitly decides to cross from an
   approved proposal into a financial command, according to ADR-041.

Risk approval does not require execution. Human intent does not override Risk.
Both conditions are required for the normal execution path.

The following are therefore invalid shortcuts:

```text
TrendContextAssessment -> Execution
ML score -> Execution
Agent consensus -> Execution
Human click -> Execution without Risk approval
Risk APPROVED -> automatic Execution
```

The last shortcut is prohibited even when Risk has approved the plan. The
human authorization boundary remains explicit.

## 12. Safe Degradation

The architecture degrades optional interpretation and fails closed on required
authority facts.

| Condition | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Required Market Data unavailable, malformed, or stale | The deterministic analysis produces its domain-specific `UNKNOWN`, `NO_SETUP`, `INSUFFICIENT`, `STALE`, or `INVALID` result; it does not invent evidence |
| Deterministic capability fails | A required capability makes the assessment unavailable/degraded; optional capability failure remains explicit and cannot create false completeness |
| ML unavailable | Deterministic analysis may continue; ML-dependent enrichment/ranking is absent and marked unavailable |
| ML stale, drifted, or out of distribution | Do not use the result for its declared purpose; retain deterministic evidence and explicit ML degradation |
| Agent/LLM unavailable | Deterministic and Risk workflows may continue when their own required inputs are available; contextual interpretation is absent |
| News/macro unavailable | Mark the context missing; do not claim event clearance or absence of event risk |
| Agent contradicts deterministic evidence | Preserve the contradiction; deterministic evidence remains unchanged |
| ML contradicts deterministic evidence | Preserve separate statistical evidence; deterministic hard exclusions and findings remain authoritative |
| Risk rejects attractive context | Stop the financial workflow; preserve the analytical result and Risk rejection |
| Risk is unavailable for required authorization | Fail closed; no execution command |
| Broker LIVE outcome is ambiguous | Use `UNKNOWN` and reconciliation semantics from ADR-029/041; never blind retry |

Optional interpretation must not be a hidden prerequisite for deterministic
analysis or Risk authorization. A degraded result must remain visibly degraded
and must not be promoted as complete evidence.

## 13. Persistence and Future Learning

The historical intelligence dataset must represent the decision process, not
only executed or profitable trades.

Where relevant, Trading OS should preserve or link:

```text
Market observation
Deterministic assessment
Attention outcome
Human review
Strategy evaluation
Strategy match
Trading opportunity
TradePlan
Risk evaluation
Execution intent / execution
Position
Exit
Outcome
```

Not every assessment reaches every later stage. The system should retain
historically useful cases such as:

- `UNKNOWN`;
- `NO_SETUP`;
- `WATCH`;
- ignored or deferred contexts;
- human-rejected contexts;
- Risk-rejected plans;
- approved but not executed plans;
- executed trades;
- stale or unavailable evidence.

These cases are not interchangeable negative labels. Their reason and stage
must remain distinguishable where the owning workflow records them.

Persisting no-trade and rejected cases reduces survivorship and selection bias
for future statistical analysis and ML. It also supports the trader's need to
learn from process quality rather than only from realized PnL.

This ADR does not define a physical database schema, feature store, or retention
implementation. It defines the lineage and semantic preservation requirement.

## 14. Ownership of Outcomes

Later financial outcomes do not retroactively rewrite earlier evidence.

For example:

```text
Contextually attractive
Risk approved
Human executed
Trade lost
```

does not imply that the original deterministic assessment was false. It means
that the context and subsequent outcome are separate historical facts.

Likewise:

```text
Poor process
but profitable trade
```

does not make the process correct retroactively.

Outcome labels may be attached after their intended horizon, with explicit
temporal references. They must not modify the original assessment, strategy
evaluation, human decision, or Risk result.

## 15. Naming and UI Semantics

API and UI contracts must preserve the distinction between analytical evidence,
proposal, financial authorization, and execution state.

The following terms must not be presented as equivalent:

```text
CONTEXTUALLY_ATTRACTIVE  !=  RISK_APPROVED
CONTEXTUALLY_ATTRACTIVE  !=  EXECUTION_AUTHORIZED
ML_HIGH_SCORE            !=  STRATEGY_MATCH
AGENT_SUPPORTS_THESIS    !=  DETERMINISTIC_FACT
RISK_APPROVED            !=  EXECUTION_AUTHORIZED
```

Exact UI design is out of scope. The semantic distinction is architectural and
must survive any API or presentation model.

## 16. Rejected Alternatives

### 16.1 One generic AI or Intelligence score

Rejected.

It mixes source facts, deterministic calculations, statistical estimates, and
interpretations. It hides contradictions, weakens provenance, and makes it
unclear which component can be challenged or overridden.

### 16.2 LLM as authoritative raw-chart market state

Rejected as the authoritative deterministic path.

Where reproducible market facts and derived structure can be calculated by
explicit rules, the deterministic path owns those conclusions. An LLM may
interpret or challenge the result, but its prose does not replace the evidence.

### 16.3 ML directly authorizes trades

Rejected.

ML evidence may rank or explain already eligible contexts. It cannot authorize a
TradePlan, override Risk, or create execution authority.

### 16.4 Agent consensus overrides Risk

Rejected.

Consensus among interpretive components does not change the authority of the
deterministic Risk Engine.

### 16.5 TradingOpportunity directly from favorable Trend Context

Rejected.

ADR-034 requires StrategyEvaluation and immutable StrategyMatch semantics before
an opportunity. This preserves explicit strategy identity and prevents weak
generic observations from becoming trader-facing candidates.

### 16.6 Persist only executed trades

Rejected.

It creates selection and survivorship bias, loses why the trader did not act,
and prevents meaningful evaluation of `NO_SETUP`, rejected, stale, or ignored
contexts.

### 16.7 Mutable historical assessments

Rejected.

Mutation destroys reconstruction, makes rule/model evolution ambiguous, and
allows future information to leak into earlier decisions.

### 16.8 Generic synchronous pipeline requiring ML and LLM

Rejected.

Deterministic analysis and Risk must remain useful and safe when probabilistic
services are unavailable, stale, slow, or degraded. Market Intelligence
orchestration may coordinate them without making them mandatory for every path.

## 17. Consequences

### Positive

- Market analysis becomes reproducible and attributable.
- Deterministic controls remain stronger than probabilistic interpretations.
- Future ML and agents can consume trusted structured evidence safely.
- Strategy and opportunity semantics remain explicit.
- Risk authority remains isolated and fail-closed.
- Human control remains visible and enforceable.
- Historical decisions, including no-trade decisions, remain useful for learning.
- Temporal integrity and no-look-ahead rules support honest PAPER evaluation and
  future ML.
- UI and API contracts can distinguish evidence, proposals, authorization, and
  execution.
- Optional probabilistic services can degrade independently.

### Costs and trade-offs

- Contracts require more producer, authority, provenance, and version metadata.
- Persistence must preserve more references and non-execution outcomes.
- Domain boundaries require explicit orchestration and promotion rules.
- Rule, strategy, model, and agent evolution creates additional versions to
  manage.
- A favorable analysis cannot shortcut directly to execution.
- Future agent and ML features require structured evidence and governance rather
  than only convenient prose or scores.

These costs are intentional consequences of capital preservation, auditability,
and human-controlled execution.

## 18. Out of Scope

This ADR does not decide:

- Trend Context pivot formulas, EMA/ATR formulas, pullback thresholds,
  extension thresholds, or timeframe defaults;
- a dedicated Trend Context ADR;
- ML algorithms, model selection, model registry, feature-store technology, or
  vector databases;
- RAG architecture;
- agent orchestration technology, agent count, prompts, or provider selection;
- news provider selection or macro-event semantics;
- detailed UI design;
- Risk rule formulas, position sizing, or account policy values;
- broker execution details, provider protocols, or reconciliation algorithms;
- physical database schemas and retention/deletion policies;
- automatic trading or autonomous position management.

Those details belong to domain specifications, future ADRs, or implementation
Stories.

## 19. Relationship to Future ADRs

### 19.1 Statistical / ML Intelligence Governance

A future ADR may be needed when ML moves beyond experiments. It may formalize:

- feature snapshot and schema contracts;
- model lifecycle and registry boundaries;
- training dataset, label, and evaluation provenance;
- calibration, uncertainty, drift, and abstention;
- shadow/PAPER operation and promotion;
- selection-bias and leakage controls;
- model permissions and non-authority over exclusions, Risk, and execution.

This ADR intentionally does not create that design now.

### 19.2 Agent / LLM Reasoning Governance

A future ADR may be needed when structured agents become a production capability.
It may formalize:

- ContextPack construction and classification;
- orchestration and capability permissions;
- structured proposal lifecycle;
- source attribution and unsupported-claim handling;
- agent/provider/model boundaries;
- human validation and safe degradation.

This ADR intentionally does not create that design now.

### 19.3 Dedicated Trend Context ADR

No dedicated Trend Context ADR is required at this stage. The accepted Trend
Context design is a domain specification with versioned rules and profiles. A
separate ADR becomes justified only if implementation reveals a durable
architectural decision not covered by this ADR and the existing Market
Intelligence, Observation, Strategy, Risk, and Execution boundaries, such as a
new service or persistence authority boundary.

## 20. Acceptance Answers

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who owns authoritative market facts? | Market Data owns normalized market facts and source/freshness metadata within its contracts; Broker Service owns provider technical facts within its contracts. |
| Who owns deterministic derived evidence? | The deterministic producer in Market Intelligence owns the result of its explicit versioned rule. |
| What authority does ML have? | Separate statistical evidence with model provenance and uncertainty; no authority over deterministic facts, exclusions, Risk, or execution. |
| What authority do agents/LLMs have? | Interpretive proposals, explanations, challenges, and questions; no authority to rewrite facts, exclusions, Risk, or execution. |
| What authority does a StrategyMatch have? | Immutable evidence that one strategy version matched one context; it is not an opportunity, plan, Risk approval, or execution command. |
| What authority does a TradingOpportunity have? | Trader-facing candidate derived from a StrategyMatch; it is not Risk approval or execution authority. |
| What authority does a TradePlan have? | Concrete human-reviewable proposal; it is not execution authorization. |
| What authority does Risk have? | Deterministic final authority for account-specific financial authorization of the exact TradePlan. |
| What authority does the human have? | Discretionary acceptance/rejection/deferment, plan authorship within workflow, and explicit execution authorization after required gates. |
| What authorizes execution? | Approved Risk evaluation plus explicit human authorization through the established Execution Domain. |
| Can ML override deterministic evidence? | No. It remains separate statistical evidence. |
| Can an agent override deterministic exclusions? | No. It may report disagreement, but cannot remove the exclusion. |
| Can a human bypass Risk through normal workflow? | No. `Risk = REJECTED` or unavailable remains a stop condition. |
| Can agent consensus override Risk? | No. Interpretive consensus does not change Risk authority. |
| What happens when ML/LLM is unavailable? | Deterministic analysis and Risk continue when their own inputs are available; optional interpretation is explicitly unavailable. |
| How is historical evidence reconstructable? | Immutable source references, evidence windows, timestamps, versions, fingerprints, lineage, validity, and decision links are preserved. |
| How is look-ahead prevented? | Inputs are bounded by the assessment cut-off; later data is attached only as a separately identified outcome/label. |
| Why preserve `NO_SETUP` and rejected cases? | To represent valid product outcomes, improve auditability, and reduce selection/survivorship bias in future learning. |
| Why does financial outcome not rewrite analytical evidence? | Analytical conclusions, decisions, and later outcomes are separate immutable historical facts. |
| Which details remain outside this ADR? | Algorithm formulas, profile thresholds, ML/agent technology, UI, Risk formulas, broker details, physical schemas, and implementation Story scope. |

## 21. Implementation Constraints

Future implementation must:

- preserve existing source-service ownership;
- preserve `IntelligenceContext`, `AnalysisExecution`, and
  `IntelligenceObservation` responsibilities;
- keep deterministic evidence versioned and reconstructable;
- keep StrategyEvaluation and StrategyMatch explicit before
  TradingOpportunity;
- keep manual and opportunity-origin TradePlans on the same Risk and execution
  path;
- keep Risk independent of ML and agents for core authorization;
- require explicit human authorization before execution;
- prevent UI labels and API fields from collapsing analytical and financial
  authority;
- record safe unavailable, stale, unknown, and rejected states rather than
  silently substituting values;
- preserve temporal cut-offs and prevent look-ahead;
- avoid introducing a new generic intelligence score or authority layer.

This ADR does not authorize production implementation by itself. A future Story
must define the approved implementation scope and tests.

## 22. Consequence for Existing ADRs

This ADR adds a cross-cutting authority vocabulary and does not rewrite
historical decisions.

- ADR-014 remains authoritative for the human-terminated decision pipeline.
- ADR-020 and ADR-021 remain authoritative for Market Intelligence orchestration,
  context classification, AI execution, degradation, and promotion.
- ADR-025 remains authoritative for immutable observations, evidence, confidence,
  validity, and traceability.
- ADR-034 remains authoritative for Observation versus StrategyDefinition,
  StrategyEvaluation, StrategyMatch, and TradingOpportunity.
- ADR-027 and ADR-047 remain authoritative for TradePlan semantics and origins.
- ADR-028 and ADR-031 remain authoritative for Risk and financial-context
  ownership.
- ADR-029 and ADR-041 remain authoritative for execution lifecycle, Risk gates,
  human authorization, idempotency, and reconciliation.
- ADR-044 remains authoritative for service trust, delegated identity, and
  ownership authorization.
- ADR-045 remains authoritative for broker-neutral technical facts and execution
  boundaries.

If a future implementation reveals a material contradiction with an accepted
ADR, implementation must stop and the conflict must be presented for explicit
human resolution.

## 23. Summary

Trading OS distinguishes analytical evidence from financial and execution
authority.

```text
Source owners own source facts.
Deterministic capabilities own reproducible derived evidence.
ML owns statistical evidence.
Agents own interpretive proposals.
Humans own discretionary decisions and explicit execution authorization.
Strategy owns setup criteria and StrategyMatch semantics.
Risk owns account-specific financial authorization.
Execution owns the authorized financial command lifecycle.
```

No lower-authority interpretation, model score, agent consensus, attractive
context, or human enthusiasm may silently rewrite higher-authority facts or
bypass deterministic Risk and human execution controls.
