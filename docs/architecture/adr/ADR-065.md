# ADR-065 - Bounded Order Book Epoch Trust Across Transport Boundaries

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Review |
| **Date** | 2026-10-19 |
| **Related Decisions** | ADR-006, ADR-057, ADR-058, ADR-063, ADR-064 |
| **Related Investigation** | Story 0130 Kraken WebSocket continuity investigation |

---

## 1. Context

Market Data persists validated Kraken Order Book snapshots and deltas in local
capture sequences. A reconnect currently marks the previous sequence
`INVALIDATED`, even when the only known failure is that the transport ended.
Replay then accepts only the currently `ACTIVE` sequence. This makes historical
states that were reconstructed before a later disconnect unavailable for
point-in-time research.

The continuity investigation established that transport termination is real and
that a fresh snapshot is required after recovery. It did not establish that a
state already reconstructed from coherent evidence before the transport boundary
was false.

The system therefore needs to distinguish:

```text
the epoch cannot receive future deltas
```

from:

```text
the historical evidence already accepted in the epoch is corrupt
```

## 2. Decision

Order Book capture sequences have three lifecycle meanings:

```text
ACTIVE
    A fresh snapshot established the epoch, continuity is currently valid,
    and accepted evidence may extend it.

SEALED
    The epoch ended at a transport or fresh-snapshot boundary. No future delta
    may extend it, but accepted evidence remains replayable only through its
    final trusted evidence timestamp.

INVALIDATED
    The epoch cannot be trusted because a provider sequence gap, persistence
    failure, malformed state, or another integrity failure prevented a proven
    reconstruction.
```

Transport termination seals the currently active epoch. A provider sequence
gap, evidence persistence failure, or equivalent integrity failure invalidates
it. A fresh provider snapshot starts a new active epoch and seals any previous
active epoch rather than merging it with the new one.

## 3. Replay Boundary

Replay remains strict and uses one epoch only.

For a sealed epoch, the eligible interval is bounded by the earliest and latest
trusted `knownAt` values persisted in that epoch:

```text
cutoff < trustedFrom       -> unavailable
trustedFrom <= cutoff <= trustedThrough -> eligible
cutoff > trustedThrough    -> unavailable
```

For an active epoch, replay may use evidence known by the requested cutoff once
the epoch's initial snapshot is known. Invalidated epochs are never eligible.

Replay never consumes evidence from two epochs, bridges a continuity gap, or
carries the old in-memory book into a new epoch. A new epoch requires its own
fresh provider snapshot.

## 4. Economic Research Semantics

The V1 historical MARKET execution contract requires a trustworthy point-in-time
book at each explicit execution cutoff. It does not consume intermediate Order
Book states during the holding interval. Therefore an entry and exit may use
different independently trusted epochs:

```text
entry at T0 -> epoch A
exit at T1  -> epoch B
```

This does not claim that A and B form one continuous stream. Any future model
that consumes intermediate book evolution must declare a separate continuous
window requirement.

`ObservedMarketOutcome`, `FeatureSnapshot`, strategy intent, costs, and P&L
remain unchanged and retain their existing ownership boundaries.

## 5. Historical Migration Policy

Existing invalidated rows are reclassified only when persisted termination
evidence proves a transport-only boundary with coherent capture metadata. The
backfill does not infer provenance from timestamps, sequence duration, or the
presence of evidence alone.

Rows with unknown termination provenance, provider-gap evidence, persistence
failure, or other ambiguity remain `INVALIDATED`. Raw evidence is not rewritten.

## 6. Alternatives Rejected

### Keep ACTIVE-Only Replay

Rejected because it treats inability to extend an epoch as proof that its prior
states were corrupt and destroys valid historical research evidence.

### Trust Every INVALIDATED Sequence

Rejected because integrity failures and unknown causes must remain fail-closed.

### Bridge Reconnect Gaps

Rejected because a missing interval cannot be reconstructed or interpolated
without evidence.

### Prevent Network Disconnects

Rejected because the correction concerns historical evidence semantics, not
remote transport behavior.

## 7. Consequences

Positive:

- transport boundaries remain explicit;
- proven historical states survive natural reconnects;
- integrity failures remain fail-closed;
- point-in-time research can use independent trusted epochs;
- replay provenance remains sequence-specific.

Trade-offs:

- sequence lifecycle has one additional state;
- replay selection must enforce sealed interval boundaries;
- historical salvage is conservative and cannot recover provenance-free rows;
- continuous-window consumers remain a future, separately declared contract.
