## 1. Context

Trading OS already contains a deterministic structural calculation inside
Market Intelligence. The current implementation is centered on
`TrendContextEngine` and includes `ConfirmedSwing`, `SwingRelation`, cutoff
handling, right-side confirmation, suppression, structural levels, breaks,
transitions, and evidence references. Stories 0062 through 0065 established
the typed evidence boundary, pure engine, capability execution, observation
integration, and StrategyEvaluation boundary around that implementation.

This implementation is authoritative for the current Trend Context behavior,
but its neutral structural primitives remain private to the Trend Context
package and engine. Future deterministic capabilities may need the same
confirmed structural evidence for structural relations, structural events,
charting, trend-line analysis, and other analytical context. Keeping the
neutral kernel permanently private to Trend Context would couple those
consumers to contextual interpretation. Creating another implementation would
create competing structural authorities.

The repository investigation therefore identified an architectural decision
before Story 0071 can be designed safely: the existing accepted structural
kernel must either remain intentionally internal to Trend Context or become a
reusable Market Intelligence capability. The approved decision is to extract
and reuse it incrementally.

This ADR records the ownership and migration decision. It does not implement
the extraction, create Story 0071, define a new persistence projection, or
change existing trading semantics.

## 2. Decision Question

Where should deterministic market structure live, and how can the existing
Trend Context structural behavior become reusable without introducing a second
authority or changing accepted semantics?

## 3. Decision

Deterministic neutral Market Structure primitives SHALL be owned by a reusable,
lower-level analytical capability inside Market Intelligence.

The existing Trend Context structural kernel is the starting implementation to
extract and reuse. Trend Context SHALL become a consumer and contextual
composer of the shared structural evidence rather than the long-term owner of
duplicated or private neutral primitives.

The migration SHALL be incremental and extraction-based:

```text
existing accepted Trend Context behavior
        -> identify neutral structural kernel
        -> extract/rehome reusable primitive
        -> make Trend Context consume that authority
        -> prove semantic regression compatibility
        -> remove old private duplicate logic
```

No second independent structural engine is authorized. A temporary adapter is
allowed during migration. Temporary duplicate execution is allowed only for
regression comparison when it invokes the same extracted kernel. Temporary
duplicate business logic is not allowed.

## 4. Ownership Boundaries

### 4.1 Market Data

Market Data remains authoritative for factual provider-neutral market evidence,
including:

- provider OHLC evidence and normalization;
- closure state and interval semantics;
- synthetic and source metadata;
- source timestamps and source identity;
- gaps, readiness, freshness, and source facts.

Market Data owns evidence acquisition and normalization. It does not own the
meaning of a confirmed swing or another structural interpretation.

### 4.2 Market Intelligence / Market Structure

Market Structure owns deterministic, provider-neutral structural
interpretation, including:

- confirmed neutral structural primitives;
- the algorithm and policy identity used to calculate them;
- structural evidence and provenance for those results;
- deterministic replay and cutoff behavior.

Market Structure is analytical meaning derived from Market Data evidence. It
therefore belongs in Market Intelligence, not in Market Data.

The first reusable authority is confirmed SwingPoint evidence. Structural
relations and structural events are eligible for later extraction, but this
ADR does not require them to move with the first extraction.

### 4.3 Market Intelligence / Trend Context

Trend Context owns contextual interpretation and composition, including:

- direction;
- regime;
- phase;
- multi-timeframe composition;
- attention semantics;
- EMA, ATR, pullback, extension, invalidation, and other Trend Context
  interpretation;
- existing relation, protected-level, break, and transition behavior until a
  later explicit migration moves those rules.

Trend Context may consume the shared confirmed SwingPoint authority and may
temporarily continue applying its existing relation and event rules. It must
not independently recalculate the same SwingPoints.

### 4.4 Strategy, Observation, Risk, and AI

StrategyEvaluation owns deciding whether evidence satisfies a versioned
StrategyDefinition. A confirmed swing is structural evidence, not a strategy
criterion. Examples such as "buy after a higher low" or "enter after a
structural break" belong to StrategyDefinition and StrategyEvaluation.

Observation Builder remains the only creator of Observations under ADR-025.
Market Structure produces analytical artifacts/evidence that may contribute to
an Observation through the existing consolidation boundary. It is not a
universal Observation creator.

Risk Domain and Trading Core remain authoritative for financial authorization,
TradePlan validation, execution intent, and execution orchestration.

AI may consume structural evidence later for explanation or interpretation. AI
must not confirm, rewrite, relabel, or override deterministic structural facts.

## 5. Dependency Direction

The intended dependency direction is:

```text
Market Data evidence
        |
        v
Market Structure
        |
        v
Trend Context
        |
        v
Observation Builder / StrategyEvaluation
```

Market Structure MUST remain usable independently from Trend Context. It must
not depend on Trend Context direction, regime, phase, attention, EMA, ATR,
pullback, MTF interpretation, or strategy criteria to calculate neutral
structural primitives.

The architecture MUST NOT become either of the following:

```text
Trend Context -> Market Structure
Market Structure <-> Trend Context
```

Trend Context can be a consumer of Market Structure while remaining a higher
level analytical capability.

Market Structure must never directly create:

- `StrategyMatch`;
- `TradingOpportunity`;
- `TradePlan`;
- a Risk result;
- an execution command.

## 6. Analysis and Orchestration Boundary

Market Structure is a deterministic capability inside the existing Market
Intelligence architecture. It participates in the existing capability and
`AnalysisExecution` boundaries rather than creating a second scan or execution
pipeline.

The expected Active Scan placement is:

```text
explicit scope
    -> Market Eligibility
    -> AnalysisExecution per eligible market
    -> requested deterministic capabilities
    -> Market Structure
    -> Trend Context and/or other consumers
    -> Observation Builder
    -> StrategyEvaluation
```

Market Structure MUST NOT change explicit scan scope, override eligibility,
launch an independent scan, or bypass the existing `AnalysisExecution` and
capability lifecycle. When requested by an analysis plan, its result should be
reusable by all declared consumers within that execution.

The existing `AnalysisExecution`, capability, artifact, and
`CapabilityResult` architecture remains the orchestration boundary. This ADR
does not introduce a parallel execution aggregate or a new generic capability
framework.

## 7. Structural Evidence Semantics

### 7.1 Confirmed-only authority

Reusable authoritative Market Structure evidence is confirmed evidence.
Story 0071 is intended to begin with confirmed SwingPoints only.

Provisional or repainting structure is not part of the initial reusable
authority. A future provisional projection requires a separate explicit
decision defining its status, correction/replacement behavior, persistence,
and consumer restrictions. Provisional evidence must never silently satisfy a
contract requiring confirmed structure.

### 7.2 Temporal honesty

For every confirmed structural point:

```text
pivotTimestamp
    = timestamp of the market candle containing the extremum

confirmationTimestamp
    = earliest timestamp at which the configured deterministic algorithm had
      sufficient eligible closed evidence to confirm that extremum
```

These timestamps are distinct concepts and may differ. A confirmed structural
fact MUST NOT be treated as known before its `confirmationTimestamp`.

For a cutoff `T`, every visible confirmed point MUST satisfy:

```text
confirmationTimestamp <= T
```

No evidence after `T` may affect the result. This invariant applies to live
analysis, historical replay, backtesting, chart rendering, future
StrategyEvaluation, structural relations/events, and future trend-line
analysis.

### 7.3 Eligible evidence

The current conservative evidence policy remains the migration baseline:

- open candles cannot create or confirm authoritative structure;
- synthetic candles cannot create or confirm authoritative structure;
- a gap affecting required evidence makes the affected candidate unavailable;
- conflicting evidence cannot be resolved by arbitrary input ordering;
- missing, stale, invalid, or insufficient evidence must remain explicit and
  must not become a neutral default structure.

Market Structure consumes provider-neutral typed evidence that preserves
closure, synthetic state, source identity, timestamps, gaps/readiness, and an
explicit cutoff. A compact readiness or facts response without the required
OHLC sequence is not sufficient structural input by itself.

### 7.4 Timeframe boundary

A basic Market Structure result is calculated for exactly:

```text
one market + one interval + one cutoff + one algorithm/policy identity
```

Market Structure does not infer arbitrary multi-timeframe meaning or silently
mix intervals. MTF composition remains a Trend Context or other explicit
higher-level capability concern.

## 8. Algorithm and Versioning Policy

The existing accepted deterministic fixed-window pivot/fractal kernel with
right-side confirmation is the migration starting point. This records an
ownership and compatibility decision, not a claim that this family is
universally optimal, profitable, or permanently fixed.

Algorithm experimentation is separate from this ADR. A future algorithm may
be introduced only as an explicitly identified and versioned policy. No
universal numeric pivot radius, separation, ATR threshold, prominence
threshold, percentage reversal, or equality tolerance is established here.
Current Trend Context profile values remain profile/configuration values and
paper-validation hypotheses, not universal market truths.

Every reusable structural result must preserve provenance equivalent to:

- algorithm identity;
- algorithm/rule version;
- policy/profile version;
- canonical parameter identity or fingerprint;
- market;
- interval;
- cutoff;
- input evidence identity or fingerprint;
- relevant source references;
- producer/capability version where applicable.

Changing structural semantics, including comparison, confirmation,
suppression, eligibility, or missing-evidence rules, must produce a
distinguishable algorithm or policy version. Historical evidence must not be
silently relabeled under a newer calculation.

The determinism invariant is:

```text
same eligible candle evidence
+ same cutoff
+ same algorithm version
+ same policy parameters
    -> same material Market Structure result
```

The material result must not depend semantically on wall-clock time, provider
request timing, cache state, unordered iteration, AI output, or mutable hidden
state. Operational metadata such as `calculatedAt` may exist but is not part of
the structural meaning.

## 9. Incremental Migration and Single Authority

The existing structural implementation in `TrendContextEngine` is the source
behavior for extraction. The extraction must identify and rehome the neutral
confirmed-point kernel rather than copy or reimplement it.

During staged migration:

```text
Market Structure
    produces the confirmed SwingPoints

Trend Context
    consumes those same SwingPoints
    and may temporarily apply existing relation/break rules
```

This is allowed. The following is not:

```text
Market Structure calculates SwingPoints A
Trend Context independently recalculates SwingPoints B
```

The foundational point authority becomes singular as soon as the first
extraction is integrated. Later Stories may move HH/HL/LH/LL relations,
protected levels, structural breaks, and transitions into reusable Market
Structure layers, but each migration must preserve the existing semantics and
remove the prior private duplicate logic.

### 9.1 Regression compatibility

The extraction must prove semantic compatibility with accepted Trend Context
behavior. The expected validation shape is:

```text
existing Trend Context fixtures
    -> extracted Market Structure kernel
    -> Trend Context consuming extracted result
    -> same accepted Trend Context result
```

Byte identity is not required where representation or provenance necessarily
changes. Semantic identity of accepted behavior is required. If fingerprints
change because provenance representation changes, the reason and resulting
identity must be explicit and tested.

Existing semantics protected during extraction include, where applicable:

- strict pivot comparisons;
- right-side confirmation;
- cutoff handling;
- open-candle exclusion;
- synthetic and gap handling;
- source/evidence provenance;
- deterministic replay;
- existing relation and equality semantics;
- existing structural break semantics.

An intentional semantic change requires a separate explicit decision and
validation scope. Story 0071 must not use extraction as an opportunity to
change trading semantics.

## 10. First Extraction Scope: Story 0071 Direction

This ADR makes the intended narrow Story 0071 designable. Story 0071 may:

- extract or rehome the neutral confirmed SwingPoint calculation from the
  accepted Trend Context kernel;
- calculate for one market, one interval, and one explicit cutoff;
- expose confirmed points only;
- preserve strict temporal honesty and provider-neutral evidence semantics;
- preserve algorithm, policy, parameter, input, and source provenance;
- expose a reusable deterministic capability/artifact through existing
  `AnalysisExecution` architecture;
- make Trend Context consume the extracted point authority;
- provide semantic regression protection for accepted Trend Context behavior.

Story 0071 must not automatically include:

- a second SwingPoint engine;
- migration of HH/HL/LH/LL or equal relations;
- migration of protected levels, structural breaks, or transitions;
- aggregate direction, regime, or phase;
- BOS/CHOCH terminology or methodology;
- trend-line analysis;
- StrategyDefinition or StrategyEvaluation changes;
- StrategyMatch, TradingOpportunity, or TradePlan changes;
- Risk or execution changes;
- AI calculation or authority;
- a standalone Market Structure database or projection;
- universal numeric pivot defaults.

Structural relations remain candidates for reusable Market Structure ownership.
The current Trend Context relation behavior remains authoritative until a
future Story explicitly migrates it. That future migration must reuse the
existing semantics rather than introduce a second enum or algorithm casually.

Protected levels, breaks, and transitions are likewise future structural
concepts, not automatic Story 0071 scope. Future terminology should use
explicit neutral deterministic event semantics; this ADR does not standardize
BOS/CHOCH.

## 11. Persistence and Observation

This ADR does not introduce a standalone Market Structure database, read model,
or event-sourcing projection.

Initial results may be calculated on demand or retained as capability artifacts
inside an existing `AnalysisExecution` when required for reuse and lineage.
Retention through existing provenance or Observation mechanisms is allowed only
where an existing consumer requires it. A future durable structural projection
requires a separate lifecycle, scale, freshness, and ownership decision.

Market Structure does not directly create an Observation. Under ADR-025,
capabilities produce artifacts or capability results and the Observation
Builder creates and consolidates immutable Observations with mandatory
evidence. A future Trend Context Observation may consume a Market Structure
artifact without changing Observation Builder authority.

## 12. Strategy and Financial Boundaries

The following meanings remain distinct:

```text
confirmed swing high/low
    = structural evidence

HH / HL / LH / LL
    = structural relation evidence, when that layer exists

buy after a higher low
    = StrategyDefinition criterion

enter after a structural break
    = StrategyDefinition criterion

high-probability setup
    = strategy or AI interpretation
```

Market Structure must not create a StrategyMatch, TradingOpportunity, TradePlan,
Risk result, or execution command. ADR-034 and ADR-048 remain authoritative for
the boundary from evidence through StrategyEvaluation and financial
authorization.

## 13. Rejected Alternatives

### Alternative A - Keep all structure permanently inside Trend Context

Rejected as the target architecture. Neutral structural evidence has reuse
value independent of Trend Context. Permanent private ownership would couple
charting, trend lines, structural analysis, and future AI context consumption to
Trend Context or force each consumer to duplicate the logic.

### Alternative B - Create a new Market Structure engine alongside Trend Context

Rejected. It would create two structural authorities, allow semantic drift, and
make it unclear which SwingPoint or relation a downstream consumer may trust.

### Alternative C - Move structural algorithms into Market Data

Rejected. OHLC is market evidence; a confirmed SwingPoint is deterministic
analytical interpretation. Moving the algorithm into Market Data would mix
source-fact ownership with Market Intelligence meaning and provider-neutral
analysis.

### Alternative D - Replace the accepted kernel immediately with ZigZag, ATR,
or prominence-based detection

Rejected for this decision. Algorithm experimentation is separate from
ownership and migration. Introducing it here would mix extraction with a
semantic change and would not establish a safer authority boundary.

### Alternative E - Move the entire Trend Context structural stack in one Story

Rejected. A one-step migration would increase coupling and regression risk.
SwingPoints, relations, events, and contextual interpretation have distinct
boundaries and may migrate incrementally while preserving one authority.

## 14. Consequences

### Positive consequences

- One structural authority is available to deterministic consumers.
- Market Structure can be reused without routing every consumer through Trend
  Context.
- Trend Context becomes a clearer contextual interpretation and MTF composition
  layer.
- Future trend-line, charting, structural relation, and event capabilities can
  consume confirmed evidence directly.
- Cutoff-aware replay and backtesting retain temporal honesty.
- Provenance and versioning make structural results explainable and comparable.
- AI can consume deterministic structure later without becoming its authority.
- Strategy, opportunity, TradePlan, Risk, and execution boundaries remain
  explicit.

### Negative consequences and trade-offs

- Extracting the kernel from `TrendContextEngine` has real refactoring and API
  migration cost.
- The staged transition introduces temporary adapters and integration
  complexity.
- Existing fixtures require regression validation before and after extraction.
- Package and representation changes may require provenance or fingerprint
  migration handling.
- Confirmed-only evidence retains inherent confirmation latency.
- The existing Trend Context implementation cannot simply be copied into a new
  capability without violating this decision.
- Relation and event migration will require additional explicit Stories.

## 15. Relationship to Existing Architecture

This ADR refines existing boundaries and does not replace them:

- **ADR-020:** places Market Structure within Market Intelligence as a
  deterministic, reusable analysis capability and preserves the shared context
  and orchestration model.
- **ADR-025:** preserves the distinction between artifacts, capability results,
  and Observations, and preserves Observation Builder as the sole Observation
  creator.
- **ADR-033:** places Market Structure inside existing Active/Passive
  `AnalysisExecution` orchestration and prohibits a parallel scanner or analysis
  pipeline.
- **ADR-034:** preserves the boundary between structural evidence,
  StrategyEvaluation, StrategyMatch, and TradingOpportunity.
- **ADR-048:** applies the evidence hierarchy, deterministic authority,
  immutable provenance, cutoff integrity, and non-authoritative AI rules to
  structural results.
- **Stories 0062-0065:** remain authoritative for the current Trend Context
  input, accepted deterministic behavior, capability/observation integration,
  and StrategyEvaluation integration. Their accepted behavior is the
  compatibility baseline for extraction; this ADR does not rewrite those
  Stories.

## 16. Scope of This ADR

This ADR decides:

- Market Structure ownership by Market Intelligence;
- the dependency direction from Market Data to Market Structure to Trend
  Context;
- extraction and reuse of the existing Trend Context structural kernel;
- the single-authority invariant;
- confirmed-only temporal and evidence semantics for the first foundation;
- algorithm/version provenance requirements;
- staged migration and semantic regression requirements;
- persistence, Observation, orchestration, strategy, Risk, execution, and AI
  boundaries.

This ADR does not decide:

- a universal pivot radius or separation;
- profitability or trading effectiveness;
- a replacement algorithm;
- the final neutral relation/event model;
- provisional structure semantics;
- standalone structural persistence;
- Trend Context refactoring details;
- Story 0071 implementation details.

## 17. Resulting Architectural Direction

The target direction is:

```text
Market Intelligence
    |
    +-- Market Structure
    |     |
    |     +-- confirmed neutral SwingPoints
    |     +-- future reusable structural relations/events
    |
    +-- Trend Context
          |
          +-- consumes Market Structure artifacts
          +-- direction / regime / phase
          +-- MTF composition
          +-- attention semantics
          +-- contextual evidence
```

The first authorized implementation direction after this ADR is a narrow
confirmed SwingPoint extraction and reuse Story. It must preserve the existing
Trend Context semantics and establish one structural authority before later
relation or event Stories are considered.
