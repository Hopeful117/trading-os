# ADR-057 - Historical Market Evidence, Known-At Semantics, and Source Retention

## Status

Accepted by the human engineer for Story 0107A implementation and integration.

## Date

2026-10-15

## Decision

Market Data owns durable historical OHLC evidence. The durable contract is
provider-neutral and is distinct from both provider payloads and the
Market-Intelligence `OhlcPoint` analytical projection.

`OhlcEvent` remains the normalized acquisition/event representation. Accepted
closed provider observations are mapped to immutable/versioned historical
evidence observations in Market Data PostgreSQL.

## Evidence Identity

The logical candle identity is:

```text
marketId + interval + openTime
```

Each accepted observation has its own immutable observation ID and deterministic
observation fingerprint. Provider identity, provider symbol, source ID,
normalization version, and origin are provenance, not canonical market identity.

Multiple observations for one logical candle are retained when material source
content differs. Provider corrections never silently overwrite prior evidence.

## Temporal Semantics

Historical evidence carries explicit `knownAt`. It means the earliest
defensible instant at which the observation may be used under its evidence
policy. `knownAt` is not derived from candle close time merely because the
candle interval has ended.

Strict observed eligibility at cutoff T requires:

```text
closed = true
closeTime <= T
knownAt <= T
synthetic = false
```

Story 0107A persists only closed, non-synthetic provider observations. Forming
candles continue to exist in live acquisition/event flows but do not enter the
V1 durable historical replay dataset.

## Evidence Origin

The durable model distinguishes `CAPTURED` from `HISTORICAL_BACKFILL`.

Captured evidence records what Trading OS acquired and accepted during normal
operation. Backfilled evidence records provider historical data acquired later;
its `knownAt` is the backfill acquisition boundary and it must not be described
as historically observed at the candle time.

Strict observed reads are repository-first and never silently fall back to a
provider. Reconstructed market-history research is a future explicit policy and
does not change the observed-replay claim.

## Storage and Boundaries

Synthetic continuity candles are not persisted as provider evidence. Gaps remain
gaps. Historical facts remain independent from Market Intelligence
interpretation, ML, Risk, and execution simulation.

The first capture path is bounded and configured by tracked market IDs and
curated intervals. General historical synchronization, outcome calculation,
replay orchestration, order-flow history, and feature datasets are outside this
decision.

## Consequences

This provides a durable source boundary for future historical context
reconstruction while requiring explicit provenance and correction handling. It
does not claim that backfilled provider history represents what Trading OS knew
at the historical time. PostgreSQL remains sufficient for the curated V1
interval set; a time-series database is not introduced.
