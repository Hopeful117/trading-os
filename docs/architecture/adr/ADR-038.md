# ADR-038 — Empirical Strategy Validation Boundary

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-22

## Context

Stories 0013–0016 delivered a strategy-agnostic pipeline, two real production
strategies, explicit two-dimensional governance (`ValidationStatus` +
`StrategyOperationalStatus`, ADR-036), and persisted definitions as the
runtime source of truth (ADR-037).

However, the meaning of `ValidationStatus.VALIDATED` was never strong enough
to be trustworthy:

* `validationEvidenceRef` is an opaque String with no defined artifact
  semantics;
* no production producer of validation evidence exists; `recordValidation(...)`
  has zero production callers;
* technical tests prove software correctness, not trading value;
* `ShadowStrategyParityMonitor` proves behavioral parity (regression safety),
  not performance or usefulness;
* the replay/backtest/metrics capabilities required for empirical validation
  do not exist in Market Intelligence V1. ADR-027 places backtesting and
  paper trading in the future extended platform.

ADR-034 already anticipated this: VALIDATED requires "accepted deterministic
validation evidence (**initially backtest evidence once Backtest exists**)".
That capability does not exist yet.

A human decision has now been made:

> Technical correctness is necessary but NOT sufficient.
> `VALIDATED` = accepted deterministic/empirical strategy-validation evidence.

Consequently a technically complete strategy may legitimately remain
`UNVALIDATED + DISABLED` for an indefinite period. This is intentional.

## Decision

### Three explicitly separated concepts

```text
A. TECHNICAL CORRECTNESS
   The evaluator and pipeline correctly implement the declared deterministic
   rules. Proven by unit/integration/proof/parity tests.
   NEVER changes ValidationStatus.

B. STRATEGY VALIDATION
   Accepted deterministic/empirical evidence that this exact StrategyId and
   version satisfies Trading OS strategy-validation criteria.
   The ONLY thing that justifies ValidationStatus.VALIDATED.
   The capability producing such evidence does not exist yet.

C. OPERATIONAL ACTIVATION
   Live authorization. Owned exclusively by StrategyOperationalStatus
   (ENABLED). A separate explicit human decision from validation.
```

### Evidence semantics

`validationEvidenceRef` is a reference to accepted strategy-validation
evidence bound to this exact StrategyId + version. It remains an opaque
reference by design — it points to an immutable evidence artifact owned
outside the aggregate. Its contract is now documented on
`StrategyDefinition.recordValidation(...)` and `validationEvidenceRef()`.

### Versioning

Strategy v1 VALIDATED does NOT imply Strategy v2 VALIDATED. Evidence is
version-bound; new versions always start with fresh governance (ADR-037).
Governance state is never copied across versions.

### Activation

VALIDATED does not imply ENABLED. Validation ("this evidence is sufficient")
and activation ("this strategy may run live") are two distinct human
decisions (ADR-034 human approval boundaries, preserved by ADR-036).

### Legacy exception

`LEGACY_OHLC_TREND` remains `UNVALIDATED + BOOTSTRAP_CONTROLLED_RUN`: an
explicitly temporary, non-valid transition exception. Shadow-parity proof
demonstrates behavioral compatibility only and MUST NEVER be used as
strategy-validation evidence.

### No Level-A shortcut

Tests passing + human review does NOT produce VALIDATED. No
"technical-validation evidence" concept is introduced; that would make the
status lie about its own meaning.

### Quant V2 boundary

Backtesting, historical replay engines, performance metrics, statistical
acceptance criteria, walk-forward analysis and paper trading are deferred to
the future Quant Research capability (ADR-027 vision). Market Intelligence V1
consumes validated strategy governance; it does not re-implement research
infrastructure prematurely. The definitive ownership of the future evidence
producer (Market Intelligence vs Quant Research vs separate service) is a
future architectural decision.

### Human authority

Evidence existing never automatically validates a strategy; validation never
automatically enables one. Both remain explicit human decisions.

## Consequences

Positive:

* `VALIDATED` regains a serious, honest meaning: an engineer can ask "what
  evidence justifies this?" and know the answer must be version-bound
  empirical/deterministic proof.
* The domain API no longer invites fake validation through casual use.
* The boundary toward the future quantitative capability is explicit.

Negative / accepted trade-offs:

* Normal production strategies validated = 0 until the future capability
  exists. `OHLC_RANGE_EXPANSION_V1` stays `UNVALIDATED + DISABLED` despite
  being technically complete. This is deliberate.
* Only the legacy fixture runs live, under its controlled-run exception,
  until then.

## Known Open Governance Questions (future decisions)

1. Empirical acceptance criteria (metrics, thresholds, sample size, period).
2. Evidence producer ownership (MI / Quant Research / separate service).
3. Revocation/invalidation: today there is no path back from VALIDATED to
   UNVALIDATED. Mitigations exist (DISABLE, RETIRED, deriveVersion resets
   governance), but revocation semantics remain an open question.
4. Backtesting requirements and engine choice.
5. Whether paper trading is mandatory before ENABLED.

## Relation to Prior ADRs

* Refines ADR-034: keeps its evidence requirement, makes explicit that it is
  empirical/deterministic and that no substitute (tests, review, parity)
  qualifies.
* Preserves ADR-035 I-1/I-14 unchanged.
* Extends ADR-036: governance dimensions untouched; adds semantic strength
  to the validation dimension.
* Preserves ADR-037 source-of-truth model entirely.

## Migration Required

NO. Documentation and contract clarification only; no schema change.
