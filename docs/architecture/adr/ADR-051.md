# ADR-051 - Account Settlement Semantics and Instrument Product Identity

## Status

Accepted for Story 0084 implementation.

## Context

Trading OS currently stores account balances as asset amounts and identifies
markets primarily by provider and symbol. That is sufficient for the existing
PAPER inventory behavior, but it cannot distinguish an owned BTC balance from
USD collateral for a BTC perpetual.

Execution mode and provider are not financial product semantics:

```text
KRAKEN != DERIVATIVE
PAPER != DERIVATIVE
```

A provider may expose several product types and a PAPER account may simulate
more than one product over time.

## Decision

Trading Core Accounts own the financial interpretation of balances through a
provider-neutral `SettlementModel`:

```text
INVENTORY
CASH
UNKNOWN
```

`UNKNOWN` is a transitional, fail-closed value for ambiguous historical LIVE
accounts. Existing PAPER accounts are migrated as `INVENTORY` because that is
the behavior they currently implement.

Market Data owns canonical instrument product identity through
`InstrumentProductType`:

```text
SPOT
LINEAR_PERPETUAL
CFD
```

Market identity is therefore scoped by:

```text
provider + symbol + productType
```

The minimum compatibility policy is:

```text
INVENTORY + SPOT              -> compatible
CASH + LINEAR_PERPETUAL       -> compatible
INVENTORY + CFD               -> incompatible
CASH + CFD                    -> incompatible
UNKNOWN + CFD                 -> incompatible
all other combinations        -> incompatible
```

Compatibility is provider-independent, deterministic, local, and fail-closed.
It is not owned by Broker Service, Risk, Challenge, or the frontend.

## Consequences

- Existing inventory PAPER settlement remains unchanged.
- CASH PAPER accounts can be created as an explicit semantic foundation, but
  the current inventory settlement path rejects them.
- Existing LIVE accounts are not classified as INVENTORY from provider name.
- Market Data can represent spot and perpetual products with the same textual
  symbol without collapsing their identity.
- `CFD` is a canonical instrument product identity because Market Data may
  expose provider-neutral CFD instruments, including cTrader symbols. Adding
  the identity does not authorize settlement; no current SettlementModel is
  compatible with CFD.
- CFD compatibility remains explicitly fail-closed until a dedicated financial
  model and accepted decision define its settlement behavior. Provider identity,
  transport environment, and execution mode do not determine compatibility.
- Derivative settlement, mark-to-market, funding, leverage, liquidation, and
  Challenge configuration remain deferred to later Stories.
- `Trade` remains the minimal Trading Core-owned PAPER position projection under
  ADR-042; no universal Position aggregate is introduced.

## Migration

The additive account migration assigns `INVENTORY` to accounts linked to a
PAPER BrokerAccount and `UNKNOWN` to all other historical accounts. The market
migration assigns `SPOT` to existing market rows and replaces the provider/
symbol uniqueness constraint with provider/symbol/product-type uniqueness.

New derivative behavior must reject UNKNOWN accounts until an explicit account
classification exists.
