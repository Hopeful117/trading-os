# Trade Plan

## Purpose

This document defines the Trade Plan business concept used throughout Trading
OS.

A Trade Plan is the authoritative business artifact describing a potential
trade before deterministic risk evaluation and execution.

It serves as the contract between Market Intelligence and the downstream
domains responsible for risk evaluation and trade execution.

---

# Definition

A Trade Plan is an immutable business object representing a complete trading
proposal generated by the Market Intelligence domain.

It contains all information required for deterministic risk evaluation.

A Trade Plan is a proposal.

It is not an authorization.

It is not an execution request.

---

# Owner

**Primary Domain**

- Market Intelligence

**Consumers**

- Trading Core
- Risk Domain

---

# Lifecycle

```text
Opportunity
        │
        ▼
Trade Plan
        │
        ▼
Risk Evaluation
        │
        ▼
Execution Intent
        │
        ▼
Broker Execution
```

A Trade Plan is created once and never modified.

Subsequent processing stages produce new business objects rather than changing
the original Trade Plan.

---

# Relationships

| Concept | Relationship |
|----------|--------------|
| Opportunity | Source of the Trade Plan |
| Risk Evaluation | Evaluates the Trade Plan |
| Execution Intent | Produced after successful authorization |
| Position | May be created after execution |

---

# Invariants

A valid Trade Plan must:

- be immutable;
- represent a single trading opportunity;
- contain all information required for risk evaluation;
- be traceable throughout the trading workflow;
- remain independent of broker-specific implementations.

Once created, a Trade Plan must never be modified.

If market conditions change, a new Trade Plan should be generated.
