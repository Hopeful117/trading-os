# ADR-020: Market Intelligence Architecture

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-29
- **Deciders:** Trading OS Architecture

---

# Context

Trading OS aims to become an intelligent trading platform rather than a simple broker interface.

Previous ADRs established deterministic responsibilities:

- Market Data Service owns market data acquisition and normalization.
- Broker Service owns broker connectivity and account operations.
- Trading Core owns business logic, risk management and dashboard orchestration.

The next architectural step is defining how Trading OS produces market intelligence.

A first approach considered exposing deterministic market analyses to the AI layer. This approach was rejected because it unnecessarily limits AI reasoning to already interpreted information.

Instead, both deterministic algorithms and AI reasoning must coexist as complementary analysis capabilities operating on a shared context.

---

# Decision

Market Intelligence is not a data provider nor an AI engine.

Market Intelligence is an orchestration capability responsible for transforming heterogeneous information into actionable market knowledge.

This capability coordinates multiple analysis engines while preserving the separation of responsibilities between deterministic business logic and AI reasoning.

---

# Architectural Principles

## 1. Shared Intelligence Context

All analyses operate on a common Intelligence Context.

The Intelligence Context represents every piece of information that may be useful during reasoning.

Possible context sources include:

- market data
- historical OHLC
- order book
- recent trades
- deterministic indicators
- news
- economic calendar
- macro events
- account information
- positions
- exposure
- risk constraints
- watchlists
- user objectives
- previous analyses

The Intelligence Context is an architectural concept rather than a single DTO.

Its purpose is to provide a consistent reasoning environment.

---

## 2. Parallel Analysis Capabilities

Trading OS distinguishes two complementary analysis families.

### Deterministic Analysis

Characteristics:

- reproducible
- explainable
- testable
- same input produces same output

Examples:

- volatility
- spread
- liquidity
- imbalance
- market structure
- risk metrics
- scoring algorithms

---

### AI Analysis

Characteristics:

- contextual
- probabilistic
- cross-domain reasoning
- hypothesis generation
- scenario exploration

AI analyses may consume:

- raw data
- normalized data
- deterministic analysis results
- historical information
- user context
- news
- macroeconomic context

Deterministic outputs enrich AI reasoning but never replace access to underlying information.

---

## 3. Analysis Independence

Deterministic engines and AI agents are independent analysis capabilities.

Neither owns the other.

Neither replaces the other.

Both contribute complementary knowledge.

---

## 4. Intelligence Orchestrator

Market Intelligence introduces an Orchestrator responsible for coordinating analyses.

Responsibilities include:

- building the Intelligence Context
- selecting analysis strategy
- selecting deterministic engines
- selecting AI agents
- managing execution order
- handling timeouts
- caching reusable analyses
- combining partial results

The orchestrator performs coordination rather than analysis.

---

## 5. Progressive Analysis Modes

The architecture supports multiple execution modes.

### Passive Mode

Characteristics:

- continuous monitoring
- lightweight analyses
- incremental updates
- limited computation budget
- persistent observations

Purpose:

Maintain market awareness.

---

### Active Mode

Characteristics:

- user-triggered
- deeper context
- broader historical analysis
- multiple AI agents
- richer reasoning

Purpose:

Provide detailed market understanding on demand.

Both modes reuse the same architecture.

Only orchestration depth changes.

---

## 6. Consolidated Intelligence

Market Intelligence produces consolidated observations rather than opaque recommendations.

Possible outputs include:

- deterministic findings
- AI findings
- alerts
- opportunities
- warnings
- confidence levels
- traceability metadata

Consumers remain free to decide how these results are presented.

---

## 7. Traceability

Every observation must preserve its origin.

Consumers must be able to distinguish:

- factual data
- deterministic computation
- AI interpretation

The architecture intentionally avoids merging these concepts into a single opaque result.

---

# Responsibilities

## Market Data Service

Responsible for:

- collecting market information
- normalization
- deterministic market calculations

Not responsible for market interpretation.

---

## News Service

Responsible for:

- financial news
- economic calendar
- macroeconomic events

Not responsible for market recommendations.

---

## Trading Core

Responsible for:

- business context
- account state
- positions
- risk
- user objectives

Not responsible for market opportunity analysis.

---

## Market Intelligence

Responsible for:

- context construction
- orchestration
- analysis coordination
- result consolidation
- intelligence publication

---

## AI Engine

Responsible for:

- contextual reasoning
- hypothesis generation
- explanation
- synthesis

The AI Engine consumes the Intelligence Context directly.

It is not limited to deterministic summaries.

---

# Consequences

Benefits:

- deterministic algorithms remain authoritative where reproducibility is required
- AI reasoning keeps access to complete context
- architecture naturally supports future multi-agent systems
- passive and active analyses share the same foundation
- clear separation between facts, calculations and interpretations
- improved explainability and extensibility

Trade-offs:

- orchestration becomes more sophisticated
- context management becomes a first-class architectural concern
- analysis execution requires scheduling and resource management

---

# Future Evolution

This architecture prepares future capabilities including:

- specialized AI agents
- adaptive orchestration
- long-running background analyses
- autonomous market monitoring
- learning from historical user behavior

without modifying the responsibilities established by previous ADRs.
