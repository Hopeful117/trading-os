# ADR-004 — Market Intelligence Architecture

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-23

## Context

Trading OS is designed as an intelligent trading assistant rather than a traditional trading platform.

Market analysis should not only occur when the user opens the application. The platform should continuously maintain market awareness while allowing deeper analysis whenever the user requests it.

Running every analysis continuously would consume unnecessary computing resources, market data bandwidth and AI inference costs.

Conversely, performing every analysis only on demand would prevent the platform from detecting important market events ahead of time.

A balanced architecture is therefore required.

## Decision

Trading OS introduces a Market Intelligence layer composed of multiple cooperating agents.

The architecture distinguishes two complementary scanning modes:

- Passive Scanner
- Active Scanner

Both scanners share a common knowledge base and continuously enrich the same market intelligence repository.

---

## Passive Scanner

The Passive Scanner continuously monitors the market environment.

Its objective is not to produce trading recommendations but to detect relevant events and maintain market awareness.

Typical responsibilities include:

- monitoring market volatility;
- detecting abnormal movements;
- identifying changes in market conditions;
- monitoring economic events;
- detecting technical alerts;
- collecting contextual information.

The Passive Scanner should operate continuously when appropriate, while remaining configurable to limit unnecessary resource consumption.

Its outputs are persisted for later reuse.

---

## Active Scanner

The Active Scanner is triggered on demand by the user.

Its objective is to perform a deeper market analysis using all available information.

The Active Scanner combines:

- market data;
- deterministic technical analysis;
- macroeconomic context;
- news;
- risk evaluation;
- AI interpretation.

Its outputs include:

- opportunity ranking;
- market summaries;
- trading scenarios;
- AI recommendations.

The Active Scanner reuses the information already collected by the Passive Scanner instead of restarting every analysis from scratch.

---

## Shared Market Intelligence

Both scanners contribute to a shared Market Intelligence repository.

This repository stores:

- detected events;
- technical observations;
- market context;
- historical alerts;
- AI interpretations;
- market metadata.

This shared knowledge allows analyses to evolve incrementally over time.

---

## Configurable Focus

Both Passive and Active Scanners support configurable market scopes.

Examples include:

- Forex only
- Crypto only
- User watchlists
- Prop trading challenge focus
- Custom market subsets

This allows Trading OS to concentrate computational resources on markets relevant to the user's current objectives.

---

## Position Monitoring

A dedicated monitoring agent may continuously observe open positions.

Responsibilities may include:

- detecting increasing risk;
- monitoring approaching news events;
- suggesting stop-loss adjustments;
- suggesting profit-taking opportunities;
- detecting deteriorating market conditions.

This agent provides recommendations only.

Trade execution remains entirely under user control.

---

## WebSocket Strategy

Real-time market subscriptions should be demand-driven.

The Market Data Service should avoid maintaining unnecessary subscriptions.

Instead:

- Passive Scanner subscribes only to required market streams.
- Active Scanner may temporarily increase subscriptions while analysing.
- Position Monitoring subscribes to markets with active positions.
- User interaction may request additional subscriptions.

Subscription management should therefore remain dynamic.

---

## Consequences

Advantages:

- reduced infrastructure costs;
- lower market data consumption;
- scalable AI processing;
- improved responsiveness;
- reusable market intelligence.

Trade-offs:

- additional orchestration complexity;
- lifecycle management of subscriptions;
- shared state management.

These trade-offs are accepted because they significantly improve scalability and long-term flexibility.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### Continuous Full Market Analysis

Rejected.

Running every analysis permanently would waste computational resources and unnecessarily increase infrastructure costs.

---

### Pure On-Demand Analysis

Rejected.

Starting every analysis from scratch would ignore previously collected intelligence and reduce system responsiveness.
