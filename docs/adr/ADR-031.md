# ADR-031 — Clarify Trade Planning Context and Risk Context Responsibilities

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-01
- **Supersedes:** None
- **Clarifies:** ADR-027, ADR-028

---

# Context

ADR-027 introduced the Trade Planning architecture and the concept of a
TradingContext used to generate account-specific Trade Plans.

At the time ADR-027 was written, the deterministic Risk Domain defined by
ADR-028 did not yet exist.

ADR-028 later introduced an explicit deterministic financial model based on
versioned snapshots:

- AccountSnapshot
- PortfolioSnapshot
- MarketSnapshot
- RuleSetSnapshot

and a dedicated Risk Evaluation Context assembled by Trading Core.

During the implementation of Story 0002, the engineering workflow identified a
responsibility overlap between ADR-027 and ADR-028.

The current TradingContext contains both:

- planning information required to generate a Trade Plan;
- financial information required to authorize a Trade Plan.

As a consequence:

- Market Intelligence attempts to collect financial facts that belong to
  Trading Core and Risk Domain;
- Trading Core receives an incomplete financial snapshot assembled outside its
  architectural boundary;
- implementation requires inventing semantics such as buying power, leverage,
  rule profiles or account exposure where no authoritative source exists.

This overlap contradicts the separation of responsibilities introduced by
ADR-028.

---

# Decision

ADR-027 remains valid.

However, the context used by Trade Planning is clarified.

The previous TradingContext becomes a TradePlanningContext whose sole purpose is
to provide the information required to construct a deterministic Trade Plan.

Financial authorization is no longer part of TradePlanningContext.

All financial authority belongs to ADR-028.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsibilities are clarified as follows.

| Component | Responsibility |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| Market Intelligence | Produce analysis, observations, opportunities and Trade Plans |
| Trading Core | Resolve user, account, planning preferences and RiskBudget |
| Market Data | Provide timestamped prices and valuation facts |
| Risk Domain | Perform deterministic financial authorization |
| Broker Service | Provide broker facts only |

Market Intelligence answers:

> "How should this opportunity be traded?"

Trading Core answers:

> "For which user, account and planning configuration?"

Risk Domain answers:

> "Can this exact Trade Plan be authorized now?"

Broker Service never participates in business decisions.

---

# TradePlanningContext

TradePlanningContext represents planning information only.

Its purpose is to support Trade Plan generation.

It should contain only information required during planning.

Example structure:

```java
record TradePlanningContext(
    UUID id,
    long version,
    Instant capturedAt,
    UUID ownerId,
    UUID tradingAccountId,
    String accountCurrency,
    RiskBudget riskBudget,
    PlanningPreferences preferences
)
```

The exact implementation may evolve.

The important architectural constraint is that the object remains a planning
context rather than a financial snapshot.

---

# RiskBudget

Trade Planning introduces the concept of a RiskBudget.

RiskBudget represents the planning envelope available for Trade Plan
construction.

It is **not**:

- account balance;
- account equity;
- buying power;
- free margin;
- effective leverage.

Instead it represents the amount of risk that planning is allowed to consider.

Example:

```java
record RiskBudget(
    BigDecimal amount,
    String currency,
    String sourceId,
    long sourceVersion
)
```

RiskBudget is later validated by ADR-028.

It is never interpreted as proof that sufficient capital actually exists.

## Authoritative Source

Trading Core owns the authoritative source of `RiskBudget` and planning
preferences because it owns authenticated, account-scoped user workflows.

For each trading account, Trading Core persists an explicitly provisioned,
immutable and versioned Trade Planning Profile. A profile contains the planning
risk envelope and the strategic preferences that may be disclosed to Market
Intelligence for Trade Plan construction.

No profile or financial value is inferred from:

- account balance or equity;
- buying power or margin;
- leverage or leverage limits;
- an ADR-028 risk profile;
- broker facts;
- test fixtures or application defaults.

An account without an explicit effective Trade Planning Profile cannot request
an account-specific Trade Plan. The operation fails closed with a controlled
configuration outcome.

Profile assignment, replacement and versioning are explicit Trading Core
operations. Historical Trade Plans retain the exact profile and `RiskBudget`
version used for their construction.

---

# Planning Preferences

Planning preferences belong to Trade Planning.

Typical examples include:

- preferred entry style;
- preferred stop strategy;
- preferred target strategy;
- planning horizon;
- preferred risk budget;
- strategic planning preferences.

Planning preferences are distinct from:

- broker constraints;
- financial constraints;
- effective risk policies.

---

# Information Removed from TradePlanningContext

TradePlanningContext must no longer contain financial snapshots or financial
authority.

Examples include:

- available capital;
- buying power;
- account equity;
- free margin;
- leverage;
- existing exposure;
- projected exposure;
- rule profile;
- risk profile;
- prop-firm rule evaluation;
- portfolio heat;
- daily drawdown;
- financial snapshot versions.

These concepts belong exclusively to ADR-028.

---

# ADR-028 Ownership

ADR-028 becomes the sole owner of:

- AccountSnapshot;
- PortfolioSnapshot;
- MarketSnapshot;
- RuleSetSnapshot;
- financial projections;
- account exposure;
- leverage;
- margin;
- buying power;
- portfolio heat;
- drawdown;
- effective rule resolution;
- deterministic authorization.

Trading Core assembles these snapshots before invoking the Risk Domain.

Market Intelligence never assembles them.

---

# Handoff between Market Intelligence and Trading Core

The handoff is simplified.

Market Intelligence provides:

- immutable Trade Plan;
- Trade Plan identity;
- planning provenance;
- references to supporting analysis.

Trading Core independently assembles the Risk Evaluation Context using the
current authoritative financial snapshots.

Risk evaluation therefore depends on authoritative financial facts rather than
planning assumptions.

---

# Consequences

## Positive

- Clear separation between planning and authorization.
- Market Intelligence no longer requires financial authority.
- Trading Core owns financial context assembly.
- Risk Domain receives complete deterministic snapshots.
- Trade Planning remains independent from broker implementation details.
- Story 0002 no longer requires invented financial semantics.
- Architecture aligns with the responsibilities introduced by ADR-028.

## Trade-offs

- TradingContext must be refactored into TradePlanningContext.
- Existing APIs may require small adaptations.
- TradePlanRiskHandoffService must consume the immutable Trade Plan rather than
  partially reconstructed financial context.
- Documentation referencing TradingContext must be updated.

---

# Migration Strategy

Migration should remain incremental.

Recommended order:

1. Introduce TradePlanningContext.
2. Introduce RiskBudget.
3. Remove financial fields from TradePlanningContext.
4. Adapt Trade Planning services.
5. Assemble financial snapshots exclusively inside Trading Core.
6. Simplify the Market Intelligence → Trading Core handoff.
7. Update ADR-027 references where appropriate.

No behavior change is expected for the deterministic Risk Domain.

---

# Alternatives Considered

## Keep the current TradingContext

Rejected.

It mixes planning and financial authorization responsibilities and requires
Market Intelligence to assemble financial information that belongs to
Trading Core.

## Move Trade Planning entirely into Trading Core

Rejected.

Trade Planning remains part of Market Intelligence because it transforms
market opportunities into executable trading proposals.

Trading Core is responsible for validating those proposals rather than
creating them.

## Duplicate financial information

Rejected.

Duplicating financial authority would create inconsistent snapshots and weaken
deterministic risk evaluation.

---

# Summary

ADR-027 remains the architectural owner of Trade Planning.

ADR-028 remains the architectural owner of financial authorization.

TradePlanningContext becomes a planning model.

RiskEvaluationContext remains the deterministic financial model.

The boundary between Market Intelligence and Trading Core is therefore
strengthened while preserving the original architecture.
