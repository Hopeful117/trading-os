# ADR-026 — Trading Opportunity Model

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-30
- **Authors:** Ludovic Brot
- **Decision Type:** Core Domain Architecture
- **Related ADRs:**
    - ADR-023 — Market Intelligence Pipeline
    - ADR-024 — Observation Model
    - ADR-025 — AI Analysis Model

---

# 1. Context

Trading OS is designed as an AI-assisted discretionary trading platform.

Unlike automated trading systems, Trading OS does not directly decide or execute trades. Instead, it continuously collects market intelligence, analyzes market conditions through deterministic and AI-powered components, and assists the trader by presenting meaningful trading opportunities.

Previous ADRs introduced the concepts of:

- Market artifacts
- Capabilities
- Capability Results
- Observations
- AI Analyses

These concepts describe how information is collected and interpreted, but they intentionally stop before defining what the trader actually consumes.

A trader does not interact directly with hundreds of observations or AI analyses.

Instead, the platform must synthesize these pieces of information into a coherent business object representing a potential trading situation.

This ADR introduces that object.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Multiple independent components continuously produce information.

Examples include:

- liquidity imbalance detection
- order book analysis
- market structure analysis
- volatility analysis
- AI interpretation
- macroeconomic analysis
- news impact
- technical indicators

Each component contributes only a small portion of the overall market understanding.

Presenting raw outputs to the user would result in:

- duplicated information
- inconsistent interpretations
- excessive cognitive load
- poor prioritization
- limited explainability

Trading OS therefore requires an intermediate business concept capable of aggregating multiple analyses into something directly actionable by the trader.

---

# 3. Decision

Introduce a new aggregate root named **TradingOpportunity**.

TradingOpportunity represents a coherent market opportunity synthesized from deterministic observations and AI analyses.

It is **not**:

- an Observation
- an AI Analysis
- a Trade
- an Order
- a Trade Plan

Instead, it represents a market situation considered sufficiently relevant to deserve the trader's attention.

---

# 4. Architectural Position

The Opportunity layer is introduced between market intelligence and execution.

```text
                    Raw Market Data
                           │
                           ▼
                     Market Artifacts
                           │
                           ▼
                     Capabilities
                           │
                           ▼
                  Capability Results
                           │
                           ▼
                      Observations
                     ┌───────────────┐
                     │               │
                     ▼               ▼
              AI Analyses      User Context
                     │               │
                     └──────┬────────┘
                            ▼
                  Opportunity Engine
                            │
                            ▼
                  Trading Opportunity
                            │
                            ▼
                     Trade Planning
                            │
                            ▼
                      Risk Validation
                            │
                            ▼
                     Broker Execution
```

This pipeline clearly separates:

- data acquisition
- deterministic interpretation
- AI interpretation
- business synthesis
- execution preparation

Each layer owns a single responsibility.

---

# 5. Decision 1 — Hybrid Business Object

## Decision

TradingOpportunity is a hybrid business object.

It combines:

- deterministic knowledge
- AI-generated knowledge

Neither source replaces the other.

Both remain explicit.

---

## Motivation

Market understanding cannot rely solely on deterministic algorithms.

Likewise, AI should never replace objective market observations.

Instead:

Deterministic services provide objective market facts.

Examples:

- imbalance detected
- support identified
- breakout confirmed
- liquidity consumed

AI services provide interpretation.

Examples:

- confidence adjustment
- scenario explanation
- market narrative
- contextual reasoning

The Opportunity combines both perspectives while preserving their origin.

---

## Consequences

TradingOpportunity becomes richer than either:

- Observation
- AI Analysis

while remaining fully explainable.

Every recommendation can be traced back to:

- deterministic evidence
- AI interpretation

---

# 6. Decision 2 — Intelligence Pipeline

## Decision

Trading OS follows a layered Intelligence Pipeline.

Each stage transforms information into a higher-level abstraction.

No stage bypasses another.

---

## Pipeline

```text
Raw Market Data
        │
        ▼
Artifacts
        │
        ▼
Capabilities
        │
        ▼
Capability Results
        │
        ▼
Observations
        │
        ├──────────────┐
        │              │
        ▼              ▼
AI Analysis      User Context
        │              │
        └──────┬───────┘
               ▼
      Opportunity Engine
               │
               ▼
     Trading Opportunity
               │
               ▼
       User Decision
               │
               ▼
     Risk Validation
               │
               ▼
      Broker Execution
```

---

## Motivation

Each layer owns one responsibility only.

For example:

Observations describe facts.

AI explains facts.

Opportunity Engine combines knowledge.

Trade Planning prepares execution.

Risk Engine validates execution.

This separation minimizes coupling and keeps each component independently evolvable.

---

# 7. Decision 3 — Opportunity Engine

## Decision

Trading Opportunities are created exclusively by the Opportunity Engine.

The Opportunity Engine orchestrates all required knowledge before producing immutable TradingOpportunity instances.

---

## Responsibilities

The Opportunity Engine:

- collects Observations
- collects AI Analyses
- collects contextual information
- applies fusion policies
- detects duplicates
- manages versioning
- invokes the Opportunity Builder

It does not perform market analysis itself.

---

## Non-responsibilities

The Opportunity Engine never:

- detects market signals
- creates Observations
- executes AI models
- calculates indicators
- places trades
- performs broker communication

Its responsibility is orchestration only.

---

# 8. Decision 4 — Multiple Opportunities

## Decision

The same set of Observations may generate multiple Trading Opportunities.

---

## Example

The market shows:

- strong bullish imbalance
- breakout
- increasing volume

These observations may produce:

- Scalping Opportunity
- Intraday Opportunity
- Swing Opportunity

Each opportunity represents a different exploitation strategy while relying on the same market evidence.

---

## Consequences

Observations are reusable.

Trading Opportunities are specialized interpretations.

The relationship is therefore:

Observation

↓

0..N Trading Opportunities

rather than:

Observation

↓

1 Trading Opportunity

---

# 9. Decision 5 — Opportunity versus Trade Plan

## Decision

TradingOpportunity and TradePlan are two distinct business concepts.

A Trading Opportunity answers:

> "There is something interesting happening."

A Trade Plan answers:

> "Here is how to trade it."

---

## Pipeline

```text
Observations
      │
      ▼
Trading Opportunity
      │
      ▼
Trade Plan
      │
      ▼
Risk Engine
      │
      ▼
Broker
```

---

## Responsibilities

Trading Opportunity contains:

- market context
- reasoning
- supporting evidence
- priority
- explanations

Trade Plan contains:

- entry
- stop loss
- take profit
- position sizing
- execution parameters

---

## Consequences

Separating these concepts prevents execution logic from contaminating market intelligence.

An opportunity may exist without ever becoming a trade.

Likewise, multiple Trade Plans may eventually be derived from the same opportunity.

# 10. Decision 6 — Opportunity Lifecycle

## Decision

A TradingOpportunity owns its own lifecycle.

Its lifecycle is independent from:

- Observations
- AI Analyses
- Trade Plans
- Trades

Each opportunity evolves according to business rules and market evolution.

---

## Lifecycle

```text
           DETECTED
                │
                ▼
           ANALYZED
                │
                ▼
             ACTIVE
           ┌────┴────┐
           ▼         ▼
     CONSUMED    EXPIRED
```

---

## State Definitions

### DETECTED

The Opportunity has just been created.

Its supporting evidence has been gathered, but the Opportunity has not yet been fully evaluated.

---

### ANALYZED

The Opportunity has been enriched.

At this stage:

- deterministic observations are complete
- AI analyses are attached
- scores are available
- explanations are generated

The Opportunity is now ready for publication.

---

### ACTIVE

The Opportunity is considered relevant.

It may appear in:

- dashboards
- scanners
- watchlists
- notifications

Only ACTIVE opportunities should normally be presented to users.

---

### CONSUMED

The Opportunity has fulfilled its purpose.

Examples:

- the trader created a Trade Plan
- the trader explicitly dismissed it
- another workflow consumed it

Consumption does not imply successful execution.

It only indicates that the Opportunity has already been processed.

---

### EXPIRED

The Opportunity is no longer considered relevant.

Expiration occurs because market conditions changed.

Expired Opportunities remain available for:

- history
- analytics
- AI learning
- auditing

---

## Motivation

Lifecycle management prevents:

- obsolete recommendations
- duplicated alerts
- stale dashboard entries

while preserving complete historical traceability.

---

# 11. Decision 7 — Opportunity Score

## Decision

Introduce a dedicated business score named **OpportunityScore**.

OpportunityScore is independent from:

- Observation confidence
- AI confidence

---

## Motivation

Three different concepts must remain separated.

### Observation Confidence

Represents confidence in deterministic market facts.

Example:

> "Breakout detected with high confidence."

---

### AI Consensus Confidence

Represents agreement or confidence produced by AI models.

Example:

> "Multiple AI analyses converge toward a bullish interpretation."

---

### Opportunity Score

Represents business priority.

It answers:

> "How interesting is this opportunity for the trader?"

It is **not**:

- probability of success
- prediction accuracy
- AI confidence
- deterministic confidence

---

## Consequences

The platform can independently evolve:

- detection quality
- AI quality
- business prioritization

without coupling these concepts.

---

# 12. Decision 8 — Opportunity Registry

## Decision

Introduce an Opportunity Registry responsible for managing active Trading Opportunities.

---

## Architecture

```text
Passive Scanner
        │
        ▼
Opportunity Engine
        │
        ▼
Opportunity Registry
        │
 ┌──────┼──────────┐
 ▼      ▼          ▼
Dashboard Alerts Active Scanner
```

---

## Responsibilities

The Registry:

- stores active Opportunities
- indexes Opportunities
- exposes search capabilities
- manages lifecycle transitions
- tracks current versions
- supports deduplication

---

## Non-responsibilities

The Registry never:

- analyzes markets
- creates Opportunities
- executes AI
- computes scores
- creates Trade Plans

It is a management component.

---

## Motivation

Separating creation from management simplifies the architecture.

The Opportunity Engine produces knowledge.

The Registry manages knowledge.

---

# 13. Decision 9 — Immutable Versioning

## Decision

Trading Opportunities are immutable.

Whenever significant information changes, a new version is created.

---

## Versioning Model

```text
Opportunity V1
      │
      ▼
Opportunity V2
      │
      ▼
Opportunity V3
```

---

## Motivation

Market intelligence evolves continuously.

Modifying an existing Opportunity would:

- lose history
- complicate auditing
- reduce explainability

Versioning preserves complete evolution.

---

## Registry Behaviour

The Registry always exposes the latest active version.

Older versions remain available for:

- auditing
- analytics
- AI learning
- historical replay

---

## Consequences

Every decision remains reproducible.

Trading history always references the exact Opportunity version used by the trader.

---

# 14. Decision 10 — Opportunity Ranking Engine

## Decision

Introduce an Opportunity Ranking Engine.

Ranking is a separate concern from Opportunity creation.

---

## Architecture

```text
Opportunity Engine
        │
        ▼
Opportunity Registry
        │
        ▼
Opportunity Ranking Engine
        │
 ┌──────┼────────────┐
 ▼      ▼            ▼
Dashboard Alerts Active Scanner
```

---

## Responsibilities

The Ranking Engine:

- prioritizes Opportunities
- applies ranking strategies
- filters Opportunities
- supports personalization
- exposes sorted views

---

## Non-responsibilities

The Ranking Engine never:

- creates Opportunities
- analyzes markets
- executes AI models
- modifies Opportunity content

---

## Motivation

Creation and prioritization evolve independently.

A new ranking strategy should never require changing the Opportunity Engine.

This separation enables future experimentation with:

- user preferences
- trading styles
- portfolio context
- adaptive ranking algorithms

without affecting Opportunity generation.

# 15. Decision 11 — Opportunity Origin

## Decision

Every TradingOpportunity shall expose an immutable **OpportunityOrigin**.

The origin records how the Opportunity entered the system.

---

## Motivation

Trading Opportunities may be produced in different contexts.

Knowing the origin improves:

- traceability
- debugging
- analytics
- future optimization

without changing the Opportunity itself.

---

## OpportunityOrigin

```java
public enum OpportunityOrigin {

    PASSIVE_SCAN,

    ACTIVE_SCAN,

    USER_REQUEST,

    SYSTEM_REEVALUATION

}
```

---

## Meaning

### PASSIVE_SCAN

Generated automatically by background market scanners.

---

### ACTIVE_SCAN

Generated during an explicit scan initiated by the user.

---

### USER_REQUEST

Generated following a direct user action.

Examples:

- analyze EUR/USD
- inspect BTC/USD
- refresh watchlist

---

### SYSTEM_REEVALUATION

Generated after the system re-evaluates an existing Opportunity because new information became available.

---

## Consequences

The Opportunity model remains generic.

Its production context remains fully traceable.

No specialized Opportunity subclasses are required.

---

# 16. Decision 12 — Global Opportunities and User Projections

## Decision

Trading Opportunities are global market objects.

User-specific information shall be stored in a separate projection.

---

## Architecture

```text
                  TradingOpportunity
                         │
          ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐
          ▼                             ▼
   UserOpportunity A             UserOpportunity B
```

---

## Motivation

An Opportunity describes the market.

It does **not** describe a specific trader.

Different users may:

- assign different priorities
- create different alerts
- maintain different watchlists
- ignore different Opportunities

Those preferences belong to user-specific projections.

---

## TradingOpportunity

Contains:

- market information
- observations
- AI analyses
- score
- explanations
- lifecycle
- version

---

## UserOpportunity

Contains:

- favorite flag
- notification status
- read status
- custom ranking
- personal notes
- watchlist membership

---

## Consequences

The same market Opportunity can be reused by every trader.

Personalization remains completely separated from market intelligence.

This architectural principle can later be reused throughout Trading OS for other shared market objects.

---

# 17. Decision 13 — Opportunity Deduplication

## Decision

The Opportunity Engine shall prevent duplicate logical Opportunities.

Equivalent market situations must generate new versions instead of new independent Opportunities.

---

## Motivation

Without deduplication, scanners could continuously generate equivalent Opportunities.

This would create:

- dashboard clutter
- notification spam
- inconsistent analytics
- poor user experience

---

## Logical Identity

Equivalent Opportunities may be identified using:

- instrument
- direction
- scenario
- timeframe
- supporting observations
- creation time window

The exact matching policy remains configurable.

---

## Consequences

Users observe one evolving Opportunity instead of many duplicated Opportunities.

Historical evolution is represented through versioning.

---

# 18. Decision 14 — Expiration Policies

## Decision

Trading Opportunities expire according to deterministic business policies.

AI may recommend expiration but cannot directly expire an Opportunity.

---

## Motivation

Market conditions evolve continuously.

An Opportunity eventually becomes irrelevant.

Expiration therefore belongs to deterministic business rules.

---

## Possible Expiration Triggers

Examples include:

- validity window exceeded
- market structure changed
- supporting observations invalidated
- scenario cancelled
- macroeconomic event
- market closed

The exact policy is configurable.

---

## AI Participation

AI may produce:

> "This Opportunity is probably no longer relevant."

However:

The Opportunity Engine evaluates deterministic policies before transitioning the lifecycle.

---

## Consequences

Business consistency remains deterministic.

AI assists decision-making without controlling business state.

---

# 19. Decision 15 — Trading Opportunity Invariants

## Decision

Every TradingOpportunity must satisfy a set of architectural invariants.

These invariants define the contract of the aggregate.

---

## Business Invariants

Every Opportunity:

- has a unique identifier
- owns a lifecycle
- owns an immutable origin
- owns an OpportunityScore
- references at least one Observation
- maintains complete traceability
- may reference zero or more AI Analyses

---

## Architectural Invariants

Only the Opportunity Engine may create Trading Opportunities.

Trading Opportunities are:

- immutable
- versioned
- historized
- globally reusable
- persisted

Trading Opportunities never contain:

- user preferences
- execution parameters
- broker-specific information

---

## AI Invariants

AI enriches Opportunities.

AI never:

- modifies history
- replaces deterministic observations
- bypasses business rules

AI contributions remain explicitly identifiable.

---

# 20. Consequences

This ADR introduces the central business object connecting market intelligence with trading decisions.

The resulting architecture provides:

- clear separation of responsibilities
- deterministic explainability
- AI transparency
- immutable market intelligence
- reusable market knowledge
- user-independent Opportunities
- scalable personalization
- complete historical traceability

Trading Opportunities become the primary object consumed by dashboards, scanners, alerts, analytics, and future AI assistants.

---

# 21. Acceptance Criteria

This ADR is considered implemented when:

- TradingOpportunity aggregate exists.
- Opportunity Engine is the only creator.
- Opportunities are immutable.
- Versioning is supported.
- Lifecycle is implemented.
- Opportunity Registry manages active Opportunities.
- Ranking Engine provides prioritized views.
- User projections are separated.
- Deduplication strategy exists.
- Expiration policies are deterministic.
- Opportunity invariants are enforced.
- Unit and integration tests validate the business rules.

---

# 22. Definition of Done

The implementation is complete when:

- all architectural decisions are respected;
- the aggregate is fully immutable;
- Opportunity creation is exclusively orchestrated by the Opportunity Engine;
- all lifecycle transitions are deterministic and tested;
- user-specific state is isolated from market intelligence;
- historical versions remain queryable;
- the architecture satisfies existing project conventions and related ADRs.
