# ADR-001 — Trading OS Vision and Product Philosophy

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-23

## Context

Trading OS is a long-term software engineering project whose objective is to build an intelligent trading platform assisting discretionary traders throughout the complete trading workflow.

The project originated from the need to improve trading discipline, reduce cognitive load, centralize market information and provide AI-assisted decision support.

Unlike traditional trading terminals, Trading OS is not intended to automate trading decisions. The user always remains responsible for validating and executing trades.

The platform should progressively evolve into an AI-assisted Trading Operating System capable of combining deterministic trading rules, market intelligence, macroeconomic context and AI-generated recommendations.

## Decision

Trading OS will be developed as a complete trading assistant rather than a single-purpose trading application.

The platform must satisfy the following principles:

- AI is a core architectural component rather than an optional feature.
- The user always remains the final decision maker.
- Deterministic trading rules always have priority over AI recommendations.
- The platform should continuously assist trading preparation, execution and review.
- Every architectural decision should support long-term extensibility.

The objective is to create a professional trading environment capable of improving trading quality through better information, discipline and decision support.

## Product Scope

The platform is intended to provide:

- Market data aggregation
- Broker connectivity
- Risk management
- Trading rules
- Market intelligence
- Economic news
- AI-assisted analysis
- Position monitoring
- Trading analytics
- Historical learning
- Dashboard and trading workspace

## Non-Goals

Trading OS is **not** intended to become:

- a fully autonomous trading bot;
- a high-frequency trading platform;
- a black-box AI making trading decisions;
- a challenge-only application dedicated exclusively to proprietary trading firms.

Passing proprietary trading challenges is considered an important use case, but not the primary objective of the product.

## Consequences

This decision impacts the entire architecture.

It justifies:

- a modular microservice architecture;
- the separation between deterministic services and AI;
- the introduction of Market Intelligence services;
- user validation before trade execution;
- continuous evolution through user feedback after the first complete version.

## Alternatives Considered

### Build a challenge-focused platform

Rejected.

Although passing proprietary trading challenges is an important objective, designing the entire platform around challenge rules would unnecessarily limit its usefulness.

### Build a traditional trading terminal

Rejected.

Existing platforms already provide execution interfaces.

Trading OS differentiates itself through intelligent decision support and market understanding.
