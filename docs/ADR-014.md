# ADR-014 — Trading Decision Pipeline

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-23

## Context

Trading OS combines multiple sources of information.

These include:

- realtime market data;
- historical market data;
- deterministic technical analysis;
- economic news;
- AI-generated interpretations;
- user-defined trading rules;
- account information;
- market constraints.

Without a clearly defined decision pipeline, responsibilities between services could progressively overlap, making trading decisions inconsistent and difficult to explain.

Trading decisions must therefore follow a deterministic and reproducible workflow.

---

## Decision

Trading OS adopts a layered decision pipeline.

Each layer is responsible for enriching the available information without replacing the previous one.

The pipeline always terminates with a human decision.

---

## Decision Pipeline

```
                Market Data
                     │
                     ▼
        Deterministic Analysis
                     │
                     ▼
          Market Intelligence
                     │
                     ▼
           AI Interpretation
                     │
                     ▼
      Trading Rules Validation
                     │
                     ▼
          Human Validation
                     │
                     ▼
            Broker Execution
```

Each layer has clearly defined responsibilities.

---

## Layer Responsibilities

### Market Data

Responsible for:

- market catalog;
- realtime prices;
- historical prices;
- market constraints;
- market state.

No interpretation is performed.

---

### Deterministic Analysis

Responsible for producing objective trading signals.

Examples include:

- support and resistance;
- trend detection;
- volatility;
- liquidity imbalance;
- order book analysis;
- market structure.

Outputs remain fully reproducible.

---

### Market Intelligence

Responsible for combining contextual information.

Examples include:

- macroeconomic events;
- historical observations;
- scanner results;
- market alerts;
- position monitoring.

This layer prepares context for AI.

---

### AI Interpretation

Responsible for:

- summarizing context;
- ranking opportunities;
- explaining detected signals;
- generating scenarios;
- identifying trade-offs.

AI interprets existing information.

It never replaces deterministic analysis.

---

### Trading Rules Validation

The Risk Engine validates:

- user-defined profiles;
- challenge rules;
- maximum risk;
- market state;
- account constraints.

Only valid opportunities may continue.

---

### Human Validation

Trading OS remains an assistance platform.

The user always validates:

- opening trades;
- modifying positions;
- closing positions.

Execution never occurs without explicit user action.

---

### Broker Execution

Broker Service executes the validated order.

It does not:

- analyse markets;
- interpret recommendations;
- perform risk calculations.

---

## Explainability

Every recommendation produced by Trading OS should be explainable.

The user should always be able to understand:

- why an opportunity was detected;
- why a recommendation was produced;
- why a trade was rejected.

Opaque decision making is explicitly rejected.

---

## Design Principles

Trading decisions should remain:

- deterministic where possible;
- explainable;
- reproducible;
- traceable;
- user-controlled.

Artificial Intelligence augments decision making but never replaces human judgement.

---

## Consequences

Advantages:

- clear responsibilities;
- explainable recommendations;
- easier debugging;
- easier AI evolution;
- safer trading decisions.

Trade-offs:

- additional orchestration;
- multiple processing stages.

These trade-offs are accepted.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### AI Makes Trading Decisions

Rejected.

Trading OS is an intelligent decision-support platform.

Human validation always remains mandatory.

---

### Single Analysis Layer

Rejected.

Separating deterministic analysis from AI interpretation improves explainability and long-term maintainability.
