# ADR-008 — News Service Responsibilities

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-23

## Context

Successful trading decisions depend not only on market prices but also on external events.

Economic announcements, macroeconomic indicators and financial news can significantly affect market behaviour.

These information sources are required by several Trading OS components:

- Trading Core
- Passive Scanner
- Active Scanner
- AI Engine
- Position Monitoring Agent

Embedding news retrieval directly into these services would duplicate responsibilities and tightly couple them to external providers.

## Decision

Trading OS introduces a dedicated News Service.

The News Service becomes the authoritative source for:

- economic calendar;
- macroeconomic events;
- financial news;
- market-impact metadata.

The service is responsible for collecting, normalizing and exposing contextual information.

Business interpretation remains outside this service.

---

## Responsibilities

The News Service owns:

- economic calendar synchronization;
- financial news aggregation;
- event normalization;
- market impact classification;
- event persistence;
- publication of contextual information.

It does not perform trading decisions.

---

## Economic Calendar

The service stores structured economic events such as:

- central bank meetings;
- CPI releases;
- employment reports;
- GDP publications;
- PMI indicators;
- interest rate decisions.

Each event should expose metadata including:

- scheduled time;
- affected currencies or markets;
- expected impact;
- event status.

---

## Financial News

The News Service aggregates relevant financial information.

Typical categories include:

- macroeconomics;
- central banks;
- geopolitical events;
- cryptocurrencies;
- equities;
- commodities.

Provider-specific payloads are mapped into a common domain model.

---

## Impact Classification

The service classifies events according to their expected market impact.

Typical levels include:

- LOW
- MEDIUM
- HIGH

Impact classification is informational.

Trading decisions remain the responsibility of Trading Core and AI.

---

## Consumers

### Trading Core

Consumes news to enforce deterministic rules.

Examples:

- prevent trading during high-impact events;
- validate user-defined trading profiles.

---

### Passive Scanner

Uses news to maintain market awareness.

Examples:

- detect upcoming events;
- enrich market context.

---

### Active Scanner

Combines news with technical analysis to produce richer market summaries.

---

### AI Engine

Consumes contextual information to generate explanations and recommendations.

---

### Position Monitoring Agent

Uses news to detect situations requiring user attention.

Examples:

- approaching high-impact announcements;
- increased risk on open positions.

---

## Synchronization

News synchronization should occur periodically.

The synchronization frequency depends on the data source and event type.

Historical events remain available for future analysis and AI learning.

---

## Design Principles

The News Service provides facts.

It does not:

- recommend trades;
- perform technical analysis;
- evaluate risks;
- rank opportunities.

Those responsibilities belong to higher-level services.

---

## Consequences

Advantages:

- centralized contextual information;
- reusable economic calendar;
- reusable financial news;
- provider independence;
- simplified downstream services.

Trade-offs:

- synchronization lifecycle;
- provider maintenance;
- event normalization complexity.

These trade-offs are accepted.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### News Retrieval Inside AI Engine

Rejected.

AI should consume normalized contextual information rather than directly collecting provider data.

---

### News Retrieval Inside Trading Core

Rejected.

Trading Core should remain focused on business logic.

Collecting and maintaining news belongs to a dedicated service.
