# ADR-003 — AI-First Architecture

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-23

## Context

The primary objective of Trading OS is to become an intelligent trading assistant capable of helping discretionary traders make better decisions.

Traditional trading platforms primarily expose market data and execution capabilities.

Trading OS aims to go further by continuously collecting, analysing and interpreting market information before presenting actionable insights to the user.

Artificial Intelligence is therefore considered a fundamental architectural layer rather than an optional feature added after the core platform.

At the same time, financial trading requires deterministic behaviour for critical business operations such as risk management and rule enforcement.

## Decision

Trading OS adopts an AI-first architecture.

Artificial Intelligence is considered a core subsystem of the platform but never replaces deterministic business logic.

The platform is divided into two complementary domains.

### Deterministic Domain

Responsible for:

- risk management;
- trading rules;
- account validation;
- market constraints;
- market state;
- position management;
- broker interaction.

Deterministic services always produce predictable and reproducible results.

---

### AI Domain

Responsible for:

- market interpretation;
- opportunity ranking;
- contextual analysis;
- recommendation generation;
- historical learning;
- explanation of detected signals.

AI may interpret deterministic outputs but never override them.

## Decision Flow

Trading decisions always follow the same sequence.

```text
Market Data
        │
        ▼
Deterministic Analysis
        │
        ▼
AI Interpretation
        │
        ▼
Recommendation
        │
        ▼
Risk Validation
        │
        ▼
Human Decision
```

Human validation always remains the final step.

## AI Responsibilities

The AI Engine may:

- explain market situations;
- summarize technical analysis;
- rank opportunities;
- combine technical and macroeconomic information;
- generate scenarios;
- provide recommendations.

The AI Engine must never:

- execute trades directly;
- bypass trading rules;
- ignore risk constraints;
- modify business data without validation.

## Deterministic Responsibilities

The Trading Core remains responsible for:

- Risk Engine;
- Trading Rules;
- position validation;
- account validation;
- broker communication;
- enforcement of challenge or user-defined rules.

These responsibilities remain entirely deterministic.

## Benefits

This separation provides:

- explainable AI;
- reproducible business logic;
- regulatory friendliness;
- safer decision making;
- easier testing.

It also allows deterministic algorithms to evolve independently from AI models.

## Consequences

Future AI improvements should focus on improving interpretation rather than replacing deterministic logic.

Every new AI capability should consume structured business information instead of directly analysing raw broker data whenever possible.

## Alternatives Considered

### AI Executes Trades

Rejected.

Trading OS is designed as an intelligent decision-support platform.

Final execution always belongs to the user.

---

### AI Embedded Inside Trading Core

Rejected.

Separating AI from deterministic services improves maintainability, explainability and long-term scalability.
